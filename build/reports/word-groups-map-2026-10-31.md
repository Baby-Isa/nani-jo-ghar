# The 38 word groups, the verbs, and the settings that teach them

For Zafar, 31 Oct 2026, step one of the brainstorm: the tables only. The groups and targets come from `research-topic-wordlists-2026-10-31.md`; the verbs from `research-verbs-grammar-2026-10-31.md`; the game styles from `research-mechanics-2026-10-31.md`. Each row has five options: a setting (a trip, a room at Nani's, a game) and what the child does there. The 12 trips from the earlier map appear as options only, not as a starting point. English only; the words are meanings for Mum to say, never invented Kutchi.

A1 and A2 are word targets for the group (the A2 total is about 1,000). Keywords are a sample, not the list.

## What the count says (first reading)

- **The count is only as fair as the options.** I gave every row five different settings, choosing for the words, not to favour any trip; even so, a setting that I thought of often scores higher. Read it as a pointer, not a measure.
- **Three settings reach 10 or more word groups:**
  - the **birthday party** (11, plus 3 verb groups): greetings, family, feelings, numbers, time, toys, music, owners, celebrations
  - the **shops and market** (11): food, clothes, colours, sizes, numbers, money, places, describing words
  - an **arcade** (10): sounds, colours, shapes, numbers, money, toys, sports, technology
  
  The first two are already designed; the arcade is new.
- **The home settings punch above their weight:** the kitchen, the evening book and Tidy up are built or designed, and each reaches 7-8 groups. **Living room visitors** (new: guests arrive at Nani's) reaches 7, including greetings, question words and owners, which are the speaking glue.
- **For verbs, one setting stands out:** the **park and playground** (7 word groups and 3 verb groups: moving yourself, play, the glue), where "Nani says, do it" fits naturally. The **toy shop** (7 and 3) is the other strong new one.
- **Low reach, but the only home for a group:** the zoo or safari (wild animals), the boat (litter: household things and the sea), the swimming pool and sports day (sports), and the school or nursery (school words). Each of these covers few groups but is that group's natural place.
