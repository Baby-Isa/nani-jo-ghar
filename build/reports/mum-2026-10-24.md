## Headline grammar
1. **The verb follows its subject's gender, and for "I" the speaker's** (your decision 1): *Aau toke ambo **dinos*** (a man) / ***dinis*** (a woman); *Aau toke **naryos / naryis*** (saw), ***narato / narati*** (see), ***nardos / nardis*** (will see); *toke* to a child, *anke* to an elder. *Nana muke ambo **dino***, *E panke ambo **dini***.
2. **"To him / us / them":** *inke* (him or her, the same), *panke*, *iloka ke*.
3. **Need is *… ji jaruri*, want is *khapeti / khapeto*** (the ending follows the thing, never the person). A question is the same words with a rising voice (*Anke chai khapeti?*).
4. **Go** (your decision 2): *vinnu aaye* = want to, *vinnu khapdo* = ought to / has to, *vinnu ji jaruri* = need to; the same for he, she, they.
5. **Can:** *Aau vinni sakati / sakanto*, *E vinni saketi / saketo*, *Iloka vinni sakanto*; can't: *Aau nati / nato vinni saka*.
6. **Every day:** *Aau roj maani kianto / kianti*, *Tu / E roj maani khaito / khaiti*, *Aai* and *Pa roj maani kianta* (Mum's; Masi's *kauta* is a noted variant: your decision 3), *Iloka roj maani kenta*.
7. **They** (your decision 4): *iloka* = they in general (one word), *hiloka* = these, *huloka* = those.
