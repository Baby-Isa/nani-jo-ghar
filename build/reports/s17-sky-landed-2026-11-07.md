# The sky run landed: 15 skies matched, judged and cut (7 Nov)

Zafar's run of the sky pack (ART-56: "less jarringly vibrant and more in theme, but still distinct and attractive") landed as
15 images under ChatGPT's own chat titles in `sources/art/s17-sky/inbox/`, no runner log. This step matched each to its run
line, judged it (the tools and my eye), cut every sky into `assets/backgrounds/s17-sky/` and wrote the redo list. Nothing is
wired: no game file points at the new folder.

**In plain words.** Every line arrived: 15 of 15, one image each, nothing I couldn't place. **13 skies are cut** (424 KB in 15
WebP files; the sun card and two tile copies among them). **9 are usable now**: the anchor (the door's sunny sky), cloudy,
raining, the sun card, and five of the bank (day wide, golden wide and square, dusk wide and square). **4 pass by eye but fail
the colour tool's sky rule by its numbers** (the three night skies on the night ceiling, the day square at the margin): they
are cut and wait on Zafar (decision 1). **2 need a redo for sure**: both edits (the farm card, the book's farm spread) came
back as whole new pictures at the wrong canvas. **None are missing.**

**The anchor passes.** SD01 is a soft mid blue fading to a peach horizon, a cream sun glow in the upper right, four big
soft cumulus with warm tops, the upper left calm; mean chroma 19.5, the brightest 5% at 35 (today's door sky: 33 and 52, a
cyan blue with a lemon sun). Side by side: `build/screenshots/s17-land-sky/anchor-vs-today-door-sky.jpg`. Its edges do not
join (the sun's haze sits on the right edge), so a cross-faded tile copy carries the farm photo game (flaw 4).

## Flaws first

0. **`checks.mjs` is red on one unit test, and the fix is not in my files.** The pixel check (`build/tools/art/artcheck.mjs`
   line 48, `isBg`) treats everything under `assets/backgrounds/` as a 1600×900 scene background, so the brief's folder
   puts 15 "new" findings on the skies (1536×1024, 1254×1254, 1472×1024, 960×720: the source sizes the plan's cut note asks
   for; the game covers or tiles them, never at 1600×900). Request, one line: exclude `assets/backgrounds/s17-sky/` from
   `isBg` (skies are not scenes), or move the folder to the plan's `assets/day/sky/`, which the pixel check skips and
   `look-rules.json` already names (then the sky rule runs in `checks.mjs` and fails the four night files and the day
   square until decision 1). Everything else in `checks.mjs` passes (the Proof).
1. **Both edits were regenerated, not edited.** SE01 came back 1672×941 (the card is 1536×1024): the house's chimney and
   windows, the hay bales, the fence posts, the farmer and all three animals moved or were redrawn, and the sky is a peach
   dusk, not the bright day asked. SE02 came back 1819×865: the trees, bushes, fence and hills moved, a cloud sits near the
   fold. Neither is cut. The redo asks for a true edit at the same canvas; if the second try moves the scene again, the sky
   goes in by code (the old sky masked above the hills, SD01 behind). Pictures: `se01-edit-vs-original.jpg`, `se02-…`.
2. **The night ceiling fails every night sky.** SN01, SB04 and SB08 sit at mean chroma 36, 38 and 40 against the night
   ceiling of 24 (`look-rules.json` `sky.times.night`), while all three fit the night combination 75 (ΔE 7-8 from Violet
   Blue; today's night, at 50, fits nothing). Violet Blue's own chroma is 23, so a sky "in deep tones of Violet Blue" can
   only pass that ceiling when it is as dusty as the swatch itself. By eye they are what Zafar asked for: a quarter softer
   than today's, soft, one colour, the moon plain. `night-vs-today-door-sky.jpg`. Decision 1.
3. **The rule flips on compression at the margin.** SB01 (a lilac cloud shadow, ΔE 18 from Seashell Pink) and SB06 (peach
   ΔE 10.7) fail as PNG and pass as their WebP cuts; SB05 fails both ways (mean 25-26 against 24, the same lilac shadow). A
   ΔE 1 move from WebP crosses the ΔE 10 line. Request: a FLAG band within about 10% of each line, so a verdict never
   turns on compression.
4. **The anchor's edges don't join**: seam ratio 16.8 (seamless is under 1.6), 12 RGB between its edge columns. The cut makes
   `sky-sunny-tile.webp` (1472×1024, the last 64 columns cross-faded into the first with `artlib.make_tileable`, ratio
   0.99) for `data/farm/photo.json` (tile 3067 at the same scale); the full 1536 file stays for the door. The night joins
   almost as drawn (2.6 RGB, ratio 4.3); its tile copy is 0.88. `sky-sunny-tile-join.jpg`, `sky-night-tile-join.jpg`.
5. **The redo block can't be built from the committed run spec**: `artblock.py`'s one-combination check (ART-58, 6 Nov, after
   this block was made) now refuses SB03 and SB07 ("Violet Blue, Blue, Ochraceous Salmon … in no one combination": it
   reads "Light Glaucous Blue" as Blue). Request: `colour_exception` on those two lines of `s17-sky.run.yaml`, or the
   checker learns the name. The redo list was validated through a scratch copy with that exception (6 lines, a block of
   21.8k chars).
6. **The cutter cannot make two of the files**: a cover crop (the 4:3 sun card) and a tile copy are not `background` job
   options, so `sources/art/s17-sky/post-cut.py` makes them after the cut and records them in `cut-data.json`. Request:
   `background` jobs take `"aspect"` and `"tile"`.
7. **`look-rules.json` `palette.sky` has no glob for the new folder**, so `checks.mjs` judges the cuts by the item rule (all
   15 pass it), not the sky rule. Request: `"assets/backgrounds/s17-sky/*-golden-*": "golden"`, `*-dusk-*`, `*night*`,
   `*: "day"`. The sky-rule numbers here come from a scratch rules copy with those globs.
8. Smaller: the squares are 1254×1254 (ChatGPT's square), kept at that size; SB06's sun touches the bottom edge (asked for
   "just above"); `artredo.py` has no check flag, so the list's check is `artblock.py --redo`.
