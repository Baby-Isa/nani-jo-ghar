# The sky run landed: 15 skies matched, judged and cut (7 Nov)

Zafar's sky run (ART-56) landed as 15 images under ChatGPT's titles. This step matched, judged and cut them into
`assets/backgrounds/s17-sky/` and wrote the redo list; nothing is wired.

**In plain words.** 15 of 15 lines arrived, all placed. **13 skies are cut** (424 KB, 15 WebP files). **9 are usable
now**: the anchor (the door's sunny sky), cloudy, raining, the sun card and five of the bank. **4 pass by eye but fail the
colour tool's numbers** (the three nights, the day square): cut, waiting on Zafar (decision 1). **2 need a redo**: both
edits came back as whole new pictures at the wrong canvas. **None missing.**

**The anchor passes**: a soft mid blue to a peach horizon, a cream sun upper right, four soft cumulus, the upper left calm;
mean chroma 19.5 / 35 against today's 33 / 52 (a cyan blue, a lemon sun): `build/screenshots/s17-land-sky/anchor-vs-today-door-sky.jpg`.

## Flaws first

1. **`checks.mjs` is red on one unit test; the fix is outside my files.** `artcheck.mjs` (`isBg`, line 48) wants every file
   under `assets/backgrounds/` at 1600×900, so the brief's folder puts 15 "new" findings on skies stored at the source size
   (the plan's cut note; the game covers or tiles them). Request: exclude `assets/backgrounds/s17-sky/` from `isBg`, or
   move the files to the plan's `assets/day/sky/` (then the sky rule fails the four nights until decision 1).
2. **Both edits were regenerated.** SE01 is 1672×941 (the card is 1536×1024): house, bales, fence, farmer and animals moved,
   the sky a peach dusk. SE02 is 1819×865: trees, bushes, fence and hills moved, a cloud near the fold. Neither is cut; the
   redo asks for a true edit at the same canvas, else the sky goes in by code. `se01-edit-vs-original.jpg`, `se02-…`.
3. **The night ceiling fails every night.** SN01, SB04, SB08 sit at mean chroma 36, 38, 40 against 24, yet all fit
   combination 75 (today's night, at 50, fits nothing). Violet Blue's own chroma is 23, so a sky in its deep tones passes
   only when as dusty as the swatch. By eye they are a quarter softer than today's, as asked. `night-vs-today-door-sky.jpg`.
4. **The anchor's edges don't join** (seam ratio 16.8; the sun's haze is on the right edge): `sky-sunny-tile.webp`
   (1472×1024, 64 columns cross-faded, ratio 0.99) is for the farm photo game; the door keeps the full file.
