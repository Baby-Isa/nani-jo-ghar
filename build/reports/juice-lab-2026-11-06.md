## Flaws first

1. **I have not heard any of these.** The sandbox has no speaker. I chose every cut by measuring it (loudness over time, where the
   energy sits, onsets), and levelled them all the same way; your ear decides. Some may be wrong in ways numbers miss (the knife on a
   glass, glass C, may ring longer than you like; flame B is a blowtorch catching, which may be harsher than a hob).
2. **No recorded tea.** Nobody I could reach has a free recording of *tea* poured into a *glass*; the closest are water from a
   bottle into a glass (A, B) and a steady stream into a jug (C). Tea and water sound the same at this level; if none feels right,
   the fallback is to record one at home (a phone, a kettle, a chai glass: two minutes) and I cut it the same way.
3. **The tray's glass is seen from above.** In chai's tray the glass is a top-down picture, so the falling drop and the crown splash
   read oddly there (see "Over chai" 2: the drop lands in a circle). The painted last drop belongs in the **word moment**, where the
   glass is side-on (effects 5-7 use that glass). For the tray, I'd use the ring and the sparkle only.
4. **Steam is white and nearly invisible on cream** (switch to the light background to see). Over the dark hob it reads well; it
   should not go over the worktop.
5. **Mixkit's licence is not CC0.** It is free for commercial apps with no credit, but it forbids making a file available on its own;
   our repository and the web build serve the files openly. Fine for this lab; before the app stores either the CC0 option wins
   or the Mixkit files move out of the public repository (decision 3 below).
6. **A short pour is the long pour cut at 0.9 s** with a fade, not its own recording.
7. One change outside the page: the art pixel check (`build/tools/art/artpixels.py`, rules v14) no longer applies its "soft rim"
   rule to the painted *light* (sparkle and burst sprites): a glow is all soft rim by design. Grey, holes, dirt and smudge still apply.

## What is on the page

### The sprites (art run s17-juice, cut)

`assets/shared/juice/`, 23 WebP with alpha (264 KB in all), named by line and cell: **sk-1..5** sparkle (a point, a small star, the
big star, a thin star, two dots), **st-1..6** steam (three full wisps, three thin), **td-1..6** tea drops (the painted last drop with its
tail, a round drop, the crown splash, one, two and three droplets), **bu-1..6** gold burst (three sizes, three light rays). Longest
side 384 px. The glows and steam were cut with a **difference key** against each cell's own measured grey (alpha = how far each pixel
is from the grey, towards white or black, the largest channel wins; the colour un-premultiplied; the grey's own mottle subtracted),
so their soft edges keep; the drops with a soft-edged key and their edge colour cleaned the same way. Nothing touched a cell's edge;
SK's sixth cell was empty, as asked. Contact sheet on dark, grey and cream: `lab/juice/contact-sheet.webp` (also at the foot of the
page). The tool: `build/tools/juice/cut_juice.py`.

### The effects (`js/shared/juice.js`, by name)

| Effect | On the page | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `Juice.sparkle` kind 1 / 2 | Sparkle 1 (one star) / Sparkle 2 (three twinkles) on a full glass | a point grows into the soft star, turns a little and fades (0.8 s); or three small twinkles one after another |
| `Juice.steam` (full / thin) | Steam 1 / Steam 2 over a pan | wisps rise, drift and fade on a slow loop until stopped |
| `Juice.drop` splash crown / dots | Last drop 1 (with a crown) / Last drop 2 (droplets only), into a side-on glass | the painted last drop falls with gravity and lands |
| `Juice.crown` | Crown splash, alone | the crown rises from the surface with two droplets |
| `Juice.burst` kind 1 / 2 | Gold burst 1 / Gold burst 2 (and a light sweep) behind the stopwatch badge | a soft burst blooms behind the badge; kind 2 adds one ray sweeping across it |

Every effect is drawn by its own name, **never through `Cook.Stations` sparkle/steam stubs** (`S.verdict` draws those by score;
filling them would bring back a verdict picture). Effects never take a tap (pointer events off). **Reduced motion** (the system
setting, or `?motion=less`): each effect fades in and out where it is, nothing moves, steam is one still wisp; checked below.

## Implementation plan (after his picks; the fast lane, one sprint step)

1. **One place plays sounds.** Move the file player from `js/shared/juice.js` into `js/shared/sfx.js` as `Sfx.file(name, {volume,
   dur})` and a looping `Sfx.fileLoop(name)` (the static check's "voice" rule: sounds only through sfx.js), with the same test-hook event
   (`njg-sfx`) as the synthesised ones and the voice gate of the sound report (3b). Preload the chosen files on the station's start.
   Delete the unchosen files and their `SOURCES.md` rows.
2. **Chai's calls** (`js/cook/stations/chai-tray.js` on this branch): the hob's pour (`Cook.sfx.pourLoop`, `:1889`, via
   `js/cook/core.js:448`) and the word moment's `pour-glass` loop (`js/shared/word-moment.js:345` and `:384`) → the chosen pour as a
   file loop; the glass full (`soft("right", 0.35)`, `:1909`) → the chosen glass sound; the knob (`Cook.sfx.click()`,
   `js/cook/kitchen-kit.js:285`) → the knob sound, then the flame 180 ms later when it turns on; the green → the chosen ding; the
   shelf tap's `cook-pop` → the chosen tap, at the tap; the late `soft("tap")` → the settle as the bottle lands; the boil-over's `puff`
   stays until he hears an alternative. Every changed call keeps its line's comment with the row ID (CHAI-87, CHAI-92).
3. **Chai's effects, by name, never through the stubs:** chai's own `fx("steam", ...)` (`:1529-1530`, `:1624`) and `fx("sparkle",
   ...)` (`:3098`, `:3195`) draw the s11 sprites today; they switch to the s17 sprites through a Phaser twin of `Juice.sparkle` and
   `Juice.steam` (same names, same timings, a Phaser tween in place of the DOM animation; `Juice.less()` makes each a fade). The
   word moment's code-drawn last drop (`js/shared/word-moment.js:407`) becomes `Juice.drop` with the painted drop (td-1); the end
   screen's gold badge and the serve's stamp (`js/cook/flow.js:1069`) get `Juice.burst` behind them.
4. **Checks:** a unit test that every sound name a game calls exists and every file in `assets/shared/sfx/` has a `SOURCES.md` row
   with a licence (SH-226's contract rule); the chai contract run and labcheck at the four sizes; the judge on the changed screens.
5. **Size and risk:** about half a day mid-tier for 1-2, half a day for 3, an hour for 4. Risk: the voice gate (no effect over the
   family's voice) must land with step 1, or the louder real sounds will mask a word.
