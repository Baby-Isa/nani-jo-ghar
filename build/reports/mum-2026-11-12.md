## Headline grammar (Mum's voice, each with its time)
1. **"With" a filling is the "of" word before the dish**, agreeing with the dish (§47): *Muke ba **chunde ja** samosa khape* (5:43), *Muke hakro **chunde jo** samoso khapeto* (11:00). The filling takes its *-e* form (*chundo → chunde*, *bataato → batate*). A side is ***sathe*** after the dish (*muke maani ghee sathe khapeti*, 28:00); in chai it stays *waari*.
2. **"And one with potato"** is ***ne hakro batate jo***, the dish left out (11:00); in full *… ne hakro batate jo samoso khapeto* (10:15).
3. **"In my chai"** is ***munji chai me*** (my, agreeing with chai), and Mum's final answer puts it as a request: *Munji chai me ba khun **wijij*** (in my chai put two sugars, 12:36). "You wouldn't say I want two sugars."
4. **"{x} times"** is ***hakre fare, ba fare, trae fare***; stir three times *trae fare firai* (15:19-15:57). Not *taano* ("at some time").
5. **Tomato** is a he-word with the plural ***tameta*** (*hakro tomato, trae tameta*, 17:31; Mum used the English *tomato* for one).
6. **Genders** by "one" (*hakro / hakri*): he: sugar, flour, potato, tomato, garlic ⚠, green peas, ginger, turmeric, cumin, salt (*mitho*), chickpea (*chano / chana*), coriander, mince, ghee ("no gender": the he-form), chaat ⚠, samosa (*samoso / samosa*), cupboard, egg (*indo / inda*). She: daal, onion, chilli (and chilli powder: the same *mirchi*), mustard seeds ⚠, cardamom, yoghurt, sev (no plural), mishkaki ⚠, black pepper (*miryo*). Not settled: corn, vegetable, sekelo, mixed, meat (not asked).
7. **"Pinch"** is ***chapti*** (*hakri chapti mitho*, a pinch of salt, 57:14); a handful is ***mut***.
8. **The wrong-order "oho"** is ***Oho*** ("like the French oh la la", 55:35).
9. **Kari and mori chai:** ***mori*** is without sugar, ***kari*** without milk (45:23-45:42); sweet chai is ***mithi chai*** (*muke mithi chai khapeti*, 46:05); *muke mori chai khapeti*. Both words recorded alone (45:20-45:26).
10. **The fixed amounts:** quarter ***pa cup***, half ***adho cup*** (heard *ardho*), three quarters ***pono cup*** (*poni* for a she-word), whole ***aakho cup***, 1¼ ***sawa cup***, 1½ ***dedh cup***, 2½ ***adhi cup***, 1¾ ***pona ba cup*** (55:58-56:42).

**Counting** (32:27-34:10): 11-19 *igyaro, baro, tero, chodo, pandro, soro, satro, airo, ogni*; 21 *ekwis*, 22 *bawis*; 90 *nevu*, 100 *so*, 1,000 *hajar*. **5-10 are unclear in the noise** (32:34-32:42): not entered; five (*panj*) is still unconfirmed.

## Decisions for Zafar ("yes to all except …")
1. **Khape in the game.** In the sitting you proposed the formal endings everywhere ("*Muke chai khapeti*", and *khapeta* for two cups or two samosas) "so they learn properly". That was your voice, not Mum's: her own takes say *khape* for two samosas and *khapeto / khapeti* for one thing; §1 has her *muke ba amba khapanta* for more than one. **Recommend:** keep *khape* until Mum is asked "two samosas, said politely" once more; the engine already says *khapeti / khapeto* when a line asks for the polite register.
2. **Salt:** Mum says ***mitho*** (a he-word); the game says *loon* (the content master, never said by Mum). **Recommend** *mitho* (the engine has it as a draft word; switching the game's salt word is a data change I can make on your yes).
3. **Black pepper:** Mum says ***miryo*** (a she-word, no singular); the game says *mari*. **Recommend** *miryo*, as for salt.
4. **The pantry card's headline:** Mum's "bring me these for chai" is ***Hi muke chai lai khani achi de***; the card says *Muke de* (T1). **Recommend** Mum's sentence with the dish, and *Muke de* only where there is no dish (the plain "bring me these" was not said).
5. **Thank you:** Mum gave *aabhar*, *tojo aabhar* (to a child) and *aabhar anjo* (to an elder) "so we have a standard", but "everybody just says thank you". **Recommend** keeping the English "thank you" (G6) and the clips for a later level.
6. **"Make each cup the way they said":** you chose the simpler *Mare ji chai banai* (make everyone's chai) in the sitting; the long *… jerte iloka ke khapeti erte* is cut too. Wired as the simple one, with the English changed to "Make everyone's chai."

## Code changes a game needs to use these (not made: js/ is the builders')
1. **"with {x}" and "and {x}" in an order (samosa, any filled dish):** Mum's sentence is one noun phrase, *Muke ba chunde ja samosa khape*, not a headline plus "with {x}". The engine says it when the game asks `Need` with the dish carrying its filling (`{fn: "Item", kind, n, with: [filling]}`); the order card's "with {x}" frame passes the filling alone, so *ja / jo* has no dish to agree with. "And one with potato" (*ne hakro batate jo*) needs a new meaning (the dish left out) once the card asks for it that way.
2. **The grown-ups' English** drops "with" (`js/core/lang/engine/index.js` `enOf` ignores an Item's `via`) and says "one times": a one-line fix each.
3. **The wrong-order card (SH-264)** needs a line key that says `intj.oh-no` (*Oho*); its clip is cut.
4. **The daar stir line** "Stir it three times, slowly." is one fixed line: Mum gave *trae fare firai* (stir it three times) but not with "slowly". Either drop "slowly" from the line (then it is Mum's sentence) or ask Mum for the whole line.
5. **Baking's three-quarter cup** (CK-72) has no Cook word id yet; *pono cup* is in the engine.
