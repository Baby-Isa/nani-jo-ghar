## Per line

**T.** Row, gaps, shadows and pills are right. Both fields lean pink (red exceeds blue by ~40; spec ~23). T-1's thick pale lip reads as a second border; T-2's is quieter but lit all round, not upper-left only. Faint window streak top left in both. Tint T-2's tray in the cut, or draw the slab in code from the spec and cut only bowls and pills.

**G1.** Plan camera, glow inside, bars at thirds. Neither is wall-free: a bevelled ledge shows inside the rim; G1-1's coals stop short of the near rim. G1-2's thinner rim is the better piece; at game size the ledge passes as a bevel.

**G2.** Layout followed exactly: points past the far rim onto the marble, collars on the near rim's outer edge (G2-2; G2-1 a touch below), 2x2 on the shelf. Band order is right: from the point meat, tomato, onion, meat (the run report is mistaken). Flaws: inner walls (strong in G2-1), G2-1's deep tray, cooked meat golden like tikka, glossy raw meat, no skewer shadow on the coals. Not the screen as-is: the game moves skewers and starts with an empty tray, never drawn. Cut raw from G2-2's band, cooked from its tray, grill from G1-2.

**C1-C5.** Magenta flat (#FD03FB ±6): all key cleanly; no pink tint; sev needs a despill. Toppings fill 70-77% of the width against a ~50% floor, so stacked raw they climb the wall: scale to ~0.7 and replace the magenta shadows with one code shadow. C5 fails on colour and form.

**E1.** Perfect circles, handles at 6 o'clock; steel reflects neutral grey. On the near-black hob the iron pan (#3A3532) merges; steel separates and matches the steel chai pan already there.

**S1.** Stages 1 and 5 right. Stage 2 is a corner curl, stage 3 passable, stage 4 wrong in both: already the finished triangle with the left corner lifting. Creases are dark seams. Draw stages 2-4 in code from the geometry file; pastry texture from S1-2 stage 1, closed samosa from stage 5.

**B1 vs B2.** At a 200 px bowl, glass shows the whole mound, a liquid crater and a whole ball; opaque shows a sliver and the wet patch barely. Glass teaches it. B1-2's crater and ball beat B1-1's bubbly pool and loaf.

**B3.** Knob, runners, sage doors, brass bars, marble band, cream enamel, closed empty glass, no grille, no text: all met. Interior amber and spot-lit; runners read as gold rods. B3-2's thinner handles match the reference.

House rules: no text, hands, oval plates or cream marble. Breaks: window streaks (T); inner walls (G1, G2).

## Changes to the full run's block

1. Tray: give the hex values and "red and blue channels within 25, no peach"; lip lit upper-left only; bowl diameter 55% of the tray's depth; tray 30% high including shadow, inset 3%; "no window-light streak".
2. Grill on magenta: "the rim is one flat steel band, no inner line, ledge or bevel; coals touch its inner edge on all four sides".
3. One skewer line on magenta: raw and cooked, horizontal, points right, plus the empty steel tray; "cooked meat deep brown with char, not golden".
4. Toppings: "pieces stop at a circle 55% of the width", no contact shadows.
5. Chutney: "matte dark herb green #4E7A2E, flat ribbons of uneven width, no gloss".
6. E1: one steel pan at 40%; drop iron.
7. Drop S1; ask only for a flat textured sheet and a closed samosa on magenta.
8. Baking: glass only, a bowl-width gap between bowls, B1-ref's shape.
9. Oven: "interior soft warm grey, not orange; runners brushed steel".
