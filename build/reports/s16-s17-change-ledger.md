## 2. Lessons from the bug fixes

Each lesson names the rows that show it and the rule or check that would prevent it next time.

1. **One level or one still was checked.** SH-178 (built and shot at L1; L2's next item cut at the row's edge), SH-169 (checked at a fold, not while the list scrolled), CHAI-43 (shot at 1280x665; a phone's room is short and wide), CHAI-33 (box measured for size, not clearance), SH-196 (once per visit, so a second round never had it), SH-206 (L1 ticks every row before the pour), CK-06 (plain pan states only). Prevention: every row's check names L1-L4 and the four sizes, a new and a returning player, and the first and second round in one visit; a contract row for any shared rule.
2. **A fix measured for one thing broke another.** SH-209 (arrow fix measured height, then moved the arrow's place); SH-185 and CHAI-43 (sized by width alone or height alone); SH-206 (two serve-time cards only); CHAI-86 (kettle higher put it off the top on phones); CHAI-80 (stream picture added, kettle still drew its own chai). Prevention: when fixing a size, measure the thing's size, place and neighbours at the four sizes before and after; a before-and-after shot with numbers.
3. **A second redraw threw away an animation.** SH-180 (4 Nov: card drawn folded with transitions off; 5 Nov: reveal by a second redraw drew the folding card folded), SH-196 (the box went with the hidden dots, so the screen lit twice), SH-211 (snap pulled the row back against code glides). Prevention: one owner for each moving thing; a check that films every frame and fails on any reversal or jump (SH-211 and SH-210 now do this).
4. **Free space sized by case, not by one rule.** SH-177/SH-145 (bubble placed beside, not against other faces, the replies or the gear), CHAI-43 (width and height fitted separately), SH-203 (CSS zoom made the flight stop 90-150 px short), SH-208/CHAI-66 (shelf centred on the wrong width). The sprint's own look-back says it: "draw the screen's free space first, then size into it." Prevention: a placed-element rule in the kit (free rectangle, one shape, one scale), tested against every face, bubble, gear and panel.
5. **The narrowest reading of his words.** SH-171 (hid the items, not the headline), SH-183 ("on top" built as above everything), SH-153 (enlarge instead of remove), SH-206 (only two serve-time cards). Prevention: the `/feedback` second reader checks the row's aim against his words; write the aim, not the mechanism.
6. **A check that catches "too early" but not "never".** CHAI-77 (contract-79 and -87 passed while the third-item light never came; contract-90 now catches it), SH-196 ("shown" counted a 36x24 px box after a 3 s pause). Prevention: pair every "no early light" check with a "light seen" check, and judge whether it is noticeable, not just present.
7. **Timing assumed an adult's pace.** CHAI-58 (green of 1.7 s at a child's pace), CHAI-77 (light gave up after 3 s; the child's pace needs 8 s), SH-184/SH-205 (pouring and the right move did not reset the idle clock). Prevention: run timing checks at a child's pace and with a pause driver (pause-after-tea).
8. **A state changed in the middle of a game.** SH-207 (the ladder moved mid-round), SH-195 (snapshot per round). Prevention: progress reads frozen at a game's start.
9. **A layout engine counted by-design scrolling as overflow.** SH-210 (side fit shrank the panel when the list scrolled), the screen-check findings on chai's s14 panel. Prevention: mark a designed scroller in markup and make every check know it.
10. **Code-drawn stand-ins read as fake.** CK-06, ART-31, CHAI-64, CHAI-80, CHAI-82. Prevention: non-negotiable 8 plus a list of code-drawn effects in the audit; use the art run first.
11. **Audio and the phone's rules.** SH-198 (no audio before a tap), SH-201 (loading clip cancelled), CHAI-87 (the sound was asked for `quiet`). Prevention: a tapgate and a loadtime check for audio, run on a throttled network.
12. **Art cut artefacts.** ART-39 (counter strip beside the glass), CHAI-79 (twelve frames swapped), soft rims (dc9a728). Prevention: the art check's rim rule and a pixel-aligned cut tool (the s17-art4 cut tool).
13. **Test artefacts mistaken for bugs.** CK-57 (the debugging banner), the random bubble in F5 (a hand-started moment). Prevention: replay without the extension first and film a real start.
14. **Process, not code.** ART-42 (the runner could not upload files it fetched), the gate overridden at the S16 publish. Prevention: codify the paste-block rule in the generator (done) and keep the gate except on his explicit word.
