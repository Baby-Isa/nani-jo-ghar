## Decisions for Zafar

Answerable "yes to all except …".

1. **The L4 card's size (CHAI-43).** The card takes the room under the faces: as wide and tall as that room allows, up to the full big card; its picture, words and speaker grow and shrink with it. 2. **The splash and effects (ART-31).** (a) Now: drop the blue splash. The water's own picture fades in with the pan's small settle and the sound.
