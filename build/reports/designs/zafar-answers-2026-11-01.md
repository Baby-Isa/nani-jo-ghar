## Rules for every game

1. **How a count finishes: REVERSED later on 1 Nov, after his chai play.** A count ticks the moment the right number is reached, and an extra tap doesn't land, in every game, for consistency; half ticks when half is poured. The text below is what he said first, kept for the record:

   *Earlier the same day:*
   - **The natural next thing,** where it is clear and obvious (hand the basket over, serve the plate, tip the bucket). Never a button.
   - **Otherwise, stillness:** the count closes about **1-1.5 s after the last tap**, not 3 s. A child waiting longer thinks "nothing's happening".
   - The value is one setting in the data (start at 1.25 s), easy to change, and the same for every game.
   - This replaces `easy-to-play.md` rule 4's "a count ends at the right number": a count no longer stops itself at the right number.
2. **Voices: not now.** For prototyping, use whatever Kutchi recordings exist, and the device voice where none exist. Casting, recording sessions and who says what come before shipping, once the words are locked down. Designs must not block on a voice.
3. **Level up only on right-first-time rounds,** in every game.
4. **One way to pay:** count out the coins and hand them over; ready-made stacks for small prices.
5. **One house for the three home games, drawn as a doll's house:** a cross-section of a two-storey house, not rooms in a row. Downstairs: the kitchen and the sitting room. Upstairs: the bathroom, Nani's bedroom and Ali's room (the play room).
6. **Snap:**
   - **a tick with each correct shot**, straight away. In testing, instant feedback works better, especially for the youngest players.
   - an instant camera that prints
   - **no darkroom** (too complicated)
7. **Shipping rules come later:** all words reviewed, every character's likeness and voice. Not now.
8. **Clothes:**
   - mostly modern, modest
   - older people somewhat more traditional, but not all traditional
   - older men: a shirt tucked into trousers
   - middle-aged adults: modern smart-casual (a quarter-zip and chinos)
   - younger people: modern casual
