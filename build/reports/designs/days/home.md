## 2. The story

1. **The reason:** Nani opens the door; a black tail whisks round a corner and a bell jingles. "Simba and Zazu are hiding! Shall we find them?"
2. **Getting there:** no ride; the camera glides into Nani's bedroom.
3. **Helping:** find the cats; take Nana his things and put back what Simba knocked down; then clean one room as Nani asks.
4. **The surprise:** the cat jumps out (or something silly is in the wardrobe instead); in the clean-up, the room goes dusky and Zazu's eyes glow.
5. **Home:** it's night; Nani says "switch off the light", the child's tap darkens the house, and the book opens.

The feeling: giggly, then cosy. The end shows the dark house and the cats curled up.
