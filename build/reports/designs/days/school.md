## 2. The story

1. **The reason:** the night before school; everyone goes to bed and Nani says when each must wake ("the bus comes at eight").
2. **Getting there:** wake each sleeper at their hour; three sleepy children get ready; the child packs the school bag at the front door; the bus.
3. **Helping:** at school the teacher makes the child her helper at the board: draw it, colour it, count them, rub it out.
4. **The surprise:** the drawing comes alive on the board (the bird flaps off); a sleeper bolts up with bed-hair.
5. **Home:** the drawing is pressed onto the child's book as a sticker; the book shows "Ali woke up at six".

The feeling: grown-up. The end shows the sticker on the book's cover.

## 5. Fun and novelty

- **The new thing:** the night running fast on a wall clock; the only game about time.
- **The joke:** the sleeper's wake gag, the same each time.
- **The collectible:** the sticker; the packed things on the book's page.
- **Rhythm:** calm, busy, quick, watching, calm. `do-it` twice (3 and 6), never back to back. About 1 + 2 + 1.5 + 0.5 + 3 minutes, **about 10 minutes**: one sitting, just.
