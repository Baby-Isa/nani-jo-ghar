## 2. The story

1. **The reason:** Nani at the door: "Ali has grown! His clothes are small." (From play 2: "Nana's kurta is torn.")
2. **Getting there:** Big Ma's room, the red cloth from the shops on her table (or, if the shops haven't been played, a short visit to the stall with her).
3. **Helping:** make Ali a new kurta; mend Nana's torn sleeve; try them on.
4. **The surprise:** Ali stuck in his old kurta (the opening joke); the size gag at the fitting.
5. **Home:** the twirl and the mirror photo; on the third play Ali steps out into the weather in the shawl or with the umbrella the child chose.

The feeling: proud ("I made Ali's kurta"). The end shows the twirl and the photo.

## 5. Fun and novelty

- **The new thing:** making something real with your hands from a heard word; it is worn the next day (Ali opens the door in it on the birthday).
- **The joke:** the size gag at the fitting (too long, too small).
- **The collectible:** the mirror photo; the offcut in Big Ma's scrap tin (the patch for a later hole).
- **Rhythm:** no two stages share a main action (tap and coins; swipe; tap and needle; tap and tap). First play about 2 minutes, second about 4, third about 4.5: well inside one sitting.
