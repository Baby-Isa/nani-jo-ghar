## 2. The story

1. **The reason:** Nani at the door: the doctor needs his helper today.
2. **Getting there:** no ride on visit 1 (on a later visit the town map's "I feel poorly" sends someone here).
3. **Helping:** one to three patients, each called from the waiting room; the child finds where it hurts on the chart, fetches the cure from the pharmacy belt, and heals it.
4. **The surprise:** each heal game's own reveal (the sneeze, the plaster going on); later, the teddy that needs a plaster too.
5. **Home:** the send-off ("better!") with an apple, never a lolly; the book shows the patient waving the plastered elbow.

The feeling: proud and kind. The end shows the patient well and waving.

## 5. Fun and novelty

- **The new thing:** the body chart with the face zoom; the cure follows the part found.
- **The joke:** the patient's over-the-top hobble and the cure's reaction (the nose's sneeze).
- **The collectible:** the patient waving in the book; later the teddy's plaster.
- **Rhythm:** the morning's five stages alternate calm (chart, belt) and hands-on (heal). One patient is about 3 minutes; the first-ever morning has one patient, about 5 minutes with the door and book. Three patients is about 11 minutes: the top of one sitting.
