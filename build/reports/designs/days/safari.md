## 2. The story

1. **The reason:** Nani at the door: it's sunny; today we're going on safari with Hannah Mami.
2. **Getting there:** pack the camera, the binoculars, the hat and water; meet Hannah Mami at the park gate.
3. **Helping:** photograph each animal she names from the jeep; find the ones hiding in the long grass.
4. **The surprise:** the giraffe's stare into the camera; an animal pops out of the grass.
5. **Home:** the child's favourite print rides home on the dashboard for Ali; the book shows the prints and who hid where.

The feeling: wonder. The end shows the group photo from the grass.

## 5. Fun and novelty

- **The new thing:** the camera on a moving road: Snap from a ride, the window ride turned into the game.
- **The joke:** the giraffe that stares straight into the lens.
- **The collectible:** the prints, and the group photo; one print for Ali.
- **Rhythm:** busy and moving, then still and quiet with a pop per find. About 3 + 3 minutes, **about 8-9 minutes in all**: one sitting.
