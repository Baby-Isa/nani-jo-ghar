## 2. The story

1. **The reason:** the door is morning; Nani says it's breakfast time and everyone is hungry.
2. **Getting there:** no ride; the kitchen is through the door. The eggs from Hannah Mami's farm are in the bowl, one brown among them.
3. **Helping:** cook each person the egg they ask for; in the golden evening, bake dinner's bread with Nani.
4. **The surprise:** the spare egg, and Nani's "I'll have it!" (eggs' funny miss).
5. **Home:** dinner served under the lamps; Ali's fried egg folded in his maani goes into the book.

The feeling: cosy and useful. The end shows the whole family at dinner with the child's bake.
