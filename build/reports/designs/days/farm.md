## 2. The story

1. **The reason:** it's sunny; Hannah Mami's hens are laying and the animals need feeding.
2. **Getting there:** pack the hat and water; the farm road names the animals.
3. **Helping:** feed them; collect the eggs; photograph them doing what Nani says; the corn stall.
4. **The surprise:** lift a hen and a chick is underneath (every round).
5. **Home:** at dusk the child calls each animal home; one egg for Ali, the rest to the pantry; the photos in the book.

The feeling: useful and proud. The end shows the animals in bed and Ali's egg.

## 5. Fun and novelty

- **The new thing:** calling the animals home by voice; the photo shoot where you make the animal do it first.
- **The joke:** the chick under the hen.
- **The collectible:** the photos in the book; the egg for Ali.
- **Rhythm:** no engine twice in a row; the busy corn stall is followed by the calm walk home. **Too long for one sitting:** eleven stages at about 1-1.5 minutes each is **about 13-16 minutes**.
