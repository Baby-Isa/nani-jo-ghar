## What the bar shows across all eleven

- **No speaking moment on visit 1** (Part B rule 4): kitchen, clinic, sewing; the home day until 1b; school until L3. Each has a designed fix that waits (the chai pilot, the teddy, the stall's handover, Ask Nana).
- **Too many new words** (about 15-25 a first visit): the farm (about 46 takes for L1-L2), school (about 50 over the ladder), town and shops (numbers to 100), the birthday (about 40). They arrive level by level, so most first plays are inside the bar.
- **More than one new engine:** the home day, the birthday and school, because three shared pieces are new (the openable, give-to and `do-it`). Proposed owners: the openable and the doll's house to the home day, give-to to the birthday, and `do-it` built once with its two smallest skins (statues and getting ready), then used by the clean-up.
- **Nothing teaches until Mum records it:** every day's deciding words are mostly "to record". The prototypes don't wait: they play on existing takes and the device voice (Zafar, 1 Nov). The school day's gate is gone: Zafar says the family says the time in Kutchi (to confirm).

## The order the days unlock in (proposed)

The kitchen and the clinic are open from the start, as they are today. After them, each day opens once every day it takes something from has been played once, so carries arrive in order. **A day at home** opens third: no travel, the house the child already knows, and it teaches the places and positions every trip leans on. **The farm** is the first trip (it teaches the bag, the ride and meeting a host, and sends eggs to the kitchen). **The pet hotel** (the adoption centre, renamed) follows on the same road with the same host, reusing the farm's animal actor and corn. **Town and shops** brings numbers, money and pay, and buys the cloth and the birthday present. **Sewing** then makes Ali's kurta from that cloth. **The birthday** comes next: it needs the shops' present and the sewing's kurta, and reuses hungry from the pet hotel. **The safari** reuses the farm's Snap kit and the home day's openable. **The beach** reuses the shops' pay and kite, and its boat day opens after the beach's three plays. **The school day** comes last: its clock needs the numbers to 12 (said in Kutchi, Zafar says; to confirm with Mum). The monsoon stays parked.
