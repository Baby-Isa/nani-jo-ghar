## 2. The story

1. **The reason:** Nani at the door: "today we're going to town"; we need to cook, and everyone has an errand.
2. **Getting there:** pack the shopping bag, Nani's purse, a mango for later and the letter; the bus into town.
3. **Helping:** send each person to the place they need; then in the market, serve at the fruit stall, shop for Nani (the big pot, not the small one) and pay.
4. **The surprise:** each person comes back out carrying something (a duck follows one home); the goat with the shopping list in its mouth.
5. **Home:** put the shopping away; the book is for Ali, the cloth goes to Big Ma's room.

The feeling: grown-up and capable. The end shows the bag unpacked and Ali with his book.

## 5. Fun and novelty

- **The new thing:** pay: the purse with two piles, counting out, the open hand, the count-back. Built here first and reused by the sewing, the beach and the farm's corn stall.
- **The joke:** each place's funny refusal on a miss; the goat eats the list.
- **The collectible:** one page per errand and per stall.
- **Rhythm:** busy (the map), then the market, then the calm put-away. About 1.5 + 3 minutes for the map and the first market round, **about 8-9 minutes on the first play**, 12-13 once three market rounds open (then the rounds split over plays).
