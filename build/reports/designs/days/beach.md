## 2. The story

1. **The reason:** Nani at the door, fanning herself: it's hot; let's go to the beach, to Masi's.
2. **Getting there:** pack the red sun hat (not the blue), the towel, the bucket and spade, the water bottle, lemons for the drink; the bus along the sea road, the sea first seen.
3. **Helping:** find the shells Masi asks for; build the sandcastle; buy the picnic at three stalls and pay; lay the picnic.
4. **The surprise:** the crab: under a shell, out of the castle's top, then stealing the last chip.
5. **Home:** one shell for Nani's shelf, a lemon drink for Ali; the castle photo in the book.

The feeling: sunny and giggly. The end shows the crab running off with the chip.

## 5. Fun and novelty

- **The new thing:** building the sandcastle by how full each bucket is (fill and stack).
- **The joke:** the crab, the same three times every visit.
- **The collectible:** the castle photo; the shell on Nani's shelf.
- **Rhythm:** calm search, building, busy stalls, calm picnic; no engine twice in a row. **The full day is too long for one sitting** (eleven stages, about 15 minutes), so it grows by play: play 1 is door, bag, ride, meet, shells, sandcastle, home, book (about 8 minutes); play 2 adds the stalls; play 3 the picnic.
