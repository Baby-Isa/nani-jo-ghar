# Zafar's notes on the kitchen and farm mock-ups (1 Nov), for the prototypes

These win over the designs (`cook-baking-eggs.md`, `farm-animals.md`, `farm-eggs-orchard.md`). The prototypes are built from these, on chai's rules: chai is the best game so far, so its onboarding, language cards, helper card, order card, timings and every rule from his chai feedback apply.

## Every game

- **One camera per screen.** Side-on for pouring, measuring and putting things together at the counter; top-down for pans and the hob; never a hybrid. Chaat comes last among the cooking games, in the baking look.
- **Make it easy to draw and replicate:** fixed slots, and animals in a few shared poses that can stand in any slot.
- **Animation is simple:** pose swaps, small bounces, steam, sliding plates, short loops of 2-3 frames. Nothing that needs 3D.
- **Mock-ups vs the game:** the prototype is checked at the four sizes before any art run; if it can't come close, say so.
- **Hosts:** Hannah Mami on the farm, not Nani.

## Eggs at breakfast

- The tray is the same dark walnut as the chai tray.
- Room in both the pot and the pan for several eggs.
- **Salt and pepper** are added: two new words.
- **Serving:** think it through with simple animations (a pose swap, a bounce, steam, the plate sliding in). Offer options; at minimum the people sit in different places and react.
- **The meal shows the time of day,** each with a large window so the sky and light show it:
  - breakfast: a round table in a breakfast nook, or a breakfast bar, morning light
  - lunch: the dining table with no cloth, plain wood, midday light
  - dinner: the dining table with a tablecloth, night
- Keep the cat.

## Baking (his turn-by-turn design)

- **Each tap tips the bag or jar a bit more.** Nani gives one instruction at a time:
  - "put a little": one tap; it stops; "that's good"
  - "put a lot": tap until it's a lot; "stop, that's enough"; more taps don't land
  - "a little more": "a little", tap, "still a little more", tap, "enough"
- **Two kinds of container:**
  - the **glass jar**, with no marks, for a little, a bit more and a lot
  - a **measuring jug with clear lines** (no numbers) for quarter, half, full, one and a half and two and a half
- Side-on, like the mock-up. The oven looks great: its door as two pictures, the naan as its own picture, steam.
- The cat stays.

## The farm (Hannah Mami)

### Feeding

- Up to four animals in an arc of about 140°, outdoors, each with its tray in front.
- The food baskets sit in the middle gap, with different foods for different animals.
- Tap a basket, then the animal's tray, the number of times asked: the right food and the right number. Each tray ticks when it's right.
- Levels go from one animal to four.
- Every animal is drawn in the same few poses, so it can stand in any slot.

### The eggs

- Up to four hens on a perch.
- Tap a hen and she hops up (one pose swap). Her eggs are under her, in the hay.
- Hannah Mami asks for "all the eggs", "half the eggs" or "three eggs from the brown hen". Big and small, and the colours, pick the hen.
- The eggs go straight into the basket; nothing is handed over.
- Keep it short. Possible extras: "under"; "careful" (a quick tap wobbles an egg); "half the eggs".

### The photo shoot

- **The intro:** the whole scene, then the head turns (left, back to the middle, right, back), then the camera comes up, at every level.
- **The viewfinder** shows a quarter to a sixth of the scene, with the edges outside blurred. Arrows round it (up, down, left, right; only left and right needed at first), plus dragging.
- **Tap the viewfinder to take the photo:** a shutter blink and a click. The item ticks on the left **straight away**.
- **The scene is mostly still, with small loops:** a hen pecking, a cow chewing, a tail swishing.
- **The end reward:** each photo prints, its word is said once more, it lands on a table of up to six, then "shabash".
- **Harder levels:** more kinds of things, smaller or harder-to-find ones, a busier scene.

### The corn stall

- A sekelo redress, all on one screen:
  - raw cobs and the cooked plate either side of the grill
  - the chutneys and dressings on a tray, each named as it goes on
- Tap to take a cob off when it's cooked; turn it once.

### Home at dusk (with hide and seek)

- **The layout:** three homes along the top half (the coop, the cow pen, the stable or goat shed) against the evening sky; the bottom half is the farmyard.
- **The animals are scattered and some are hiding:**
  - a hen chilling in a bucket of water
  - one under the hay, one under an overturned wheelbarrow, one behind the tree
  - some in the wrong home at first (the chickens perched on the stable gate)
- **The chicks** are small and hide well.
- **Hide and seek's openables:** tap the hay, the wheelbarrow or the bucket and someone may be under it. Then tap the animal, then its home.
- Three homes and three kinds of animal; keep it to one screen.

### Plant, water, grow, then pick (one game)

- **One row of four plots:** plant the sapling, water it, a short time-lapse grows it, then pick what you sowed into the basket.
- "Pick two lemons": which plant, and how many.
- Hannah Mami hosts, not Nani; the visual is simplified.
