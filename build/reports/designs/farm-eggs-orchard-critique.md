## Findings, most serious first

### 1. MUST: a child with no Kutchi wins A and C1 by fishing, and the leak tables don't see it
- **Place:** §3A "Miss reactions" (the hen "nudges the egg back ... it rolls home"; "the egg wobbles in the basket and hops back to its nest"), §3C1 question 3 ("a wrong pick is the goat's ... the wrong one leaves the basket"), the shared rule "extras are handed back at the give ... that row redoes on the spot", and both leak tables' "Eliminating: No".
- **Problem:** a wrong-hen egg and a wrong-plant fruit are rejected the instant they are taken. So "which hen" is won by taking one egg from each hen until one stays (two to five tries, no cost, no first-go rule anywhere in the design). "How many" is won through the give: take one, tap Hannah Mami; if the row doesn't tick, take one more, tap her again, until it ticks. The design says too many are handed back "with the number said again" and says nothing about too few, so the give is a counter the child can probe. The blind odds (12% at L1, 0.1% at L2) assume one blind try per ask; with free retries and instant feedback the learned-save bot wins close to every round from L2. The lessons say ticks mustn't let a child fish (§1, C1017 P62-63) and the sibling designs all carry a first-go rule (`farm-animals.md` B1 "the row ticks gold only first go"; `clinic-every-part.md` "ticks only on a right first try"; `hide-and-seek.md` decision 5; `party-gifts.md` "never ticked as a way of fishing"). Cook's own `count.js` already does it ("a spoon of the wrong one costs the accuracy badge").
- **Evidence:** the design's own §3A and §3C1 text; UX §17 ("the first placement is what's scored ... without letting them fish for the tick").
- **Change:** one rule, written in §3's shared rules and in the leak tables: nothing is judged item by item; the whole basket is judged once at the give; a give with the wrong hen, plant or count hands everything back with the ask said again, and the row then ticks plain, not gold, and moves no word's ladder; only a first-give-right row ticks gold and counts for the leak bot. Keep the hen's nudge and the goat's munch as the *give's* funny reactions (the goat takes the wrong ones out of the basket as Hannah Mami hands it back), not as instant verdicts. Re-run the blind odds as "first give right".

### 2. MUST: two shared count rules were written the same night, both reversing Zafar's rule by assumption
- **Place:** §3 shared rules (decisions 2 and 3: "counts never end by themselves ... the step closes when the child gives"; "the give ... belongs in the shared count, so every count game gets it"), §10 open question 1.
- **Problem:** (a) `farm-animals.md` A1, the other half of the same farm day, settles the same shared rule differently ("the step closes when the child turns to the next thing, or after 2.5 s of stillness ... this is the place to settle it for every count game"). Two designs, one night, one engine (`count-to-a-number.md`), two closes. That is the lessons' "an everywhere rule fixed in one game" (§13) before a line is built, and non-negotiable 6 (the same shared pieces everywhere). (b) Both reverse easy-to-play rule 4, which is Zafar's own word (22 Oct: "a count ends at the right number and an extra tap doesn't land"), and this design builds all three games on the reversal as "assumed yes". CLAUDE.md: the newest word from Zafar wins, and a clash with him is put to him, not assumed. (c) A tap on the asker that closes every step is a Done button in the world unless it is also what the story needs; the design's own lessons line (§4: "no Next and no Done ... each step moves on by itself") is the one it marks "passed".
- **Evidence:** `easy-to-play.md` rule 4; `farm-animals.md` §3 A1 "How a count closes" and §6 ("it gains the close on stillness or the next tap rule here"); `count-to-a-number.md` ("it can be taken back until Done" is the older wording).
- **Change:** take the count close out of both farm designs and make it one numbered question to Zafar, with the leak reason stated plainly (an auto-stop teaches the number by waiting) and two candidate rules (close on stillness or the next tap, as farm-animals; or close on the give). Whichever he picks goes into `count-to-a-number.md` and `easy-to-play.md` the same day, and both farm designs cite it. Until then this design should say which rule it falls back to if he keeps rule 4 (the number heard, the extra tap not landing, the row judged at the auto-stop).

### 3. MUST: at C2 L3 the amount words never decide, because too much water always grows the plant
- **Place:** §3C2 ladder L3 ("too much: a funny puddle splash, and the plant still grows"), question 2 ("how much water from L3").
- **Problem:** if over-pouring always succeeds, "a little" (*thorok*), "a whole can" (*aako*) and "more" (*wadhare*) are beaten by holding the can until it is empty on every ask. That is winning by waiting (NN4) on the one level where C2 claims language. The design cites "baking's rule" (no fill line), but baking's consequences work both ways (too much flour puffs a cloud and the dough fails, research §14).
- **Evidence:** the design's own L3 text; `research-other-games` §14 ("consequences, not verdicts").
- **Change:** too much is also a miss with a funny consequence that redoes the step (the seed floats up on the puddle and has to be planted again, or the sprout flops and Hannah Mami says *bas* ✓ and tips the water off). Say the thresholds in data (short hold, long hold, two holds) so the builder has no question.

Name what the hen needs that the piece lacks (a content layer the child can take from, one item at a time) as a row for the shared-layer owner.

### 7. MUST: the reveal the whole eggs game rests on doesn't read at phone height
- **Place:** §7 layout ("the lift moves a hen up about 40 px ... the eggs are drawn big (at least 44 px)"), §7 cameras ("hens, eggs, nests and the chick all in E"), §6 ("settles back by itself after the pick or 4 s").
- **Problem:** in a front-on eye-level view a hen sitting on a nest hides what is under her; lifted 40 px she still covers 44 px eggs, so the count the child must make is behind her body. At 275 high there is no room to lift her further (bubbles and the card pop are above the hens). And the hen settles after 4 s: a three-year-old taking three eggs one at a time finds the hen back on the nest mid-count and must lift her again. Farm-animals solved the same problem for its troughs by drawing them in F view so cobs can be counted.
- **Evidence:** art bible §3 (F view: "the item's top is just visible"); `farm-animals.md` §7 ("troughs ... in F view (about 10° down), so the cobs in a trough can be counted"); lessons §6 ("on phones, items and faces stay big").
- **Change:** nests in F view like the troughs, and the hen hops sideways off the nest (a flap to the side) rather than up, so the eggs lie in the open and the lift stays inside the frame at every size. The lifted state holds while the ask is open (until the give or a tap on another hen), never on a timer. Give the 740x320 numbers as well as 915x275.

### 8. SHOULD: the corn stall is three times sekelo's load at L4

## Verdict

The eggs game is the right shape and has the day's best joke (the chick riding the hen down), the goat is a good funny miss, the word table is honest and nothing in it is invented. But the design is not ready for Zafar. Its leak tables miss the leak that matters: with instant per-item rejection and a give that can be probed, a child with no Kutchi wins A and C1 by fishing, and the design carries no first-go rule while every sibling design does. It then settles the shared count rule for every count game in a way that reverses Zafar's own 22 Oct rule and contradicts the other farm design written the same night; that is one question for him, not two assumptions. C2's only language level is beaten by holding the can. The hen's lift duplicates hide and seek's shared openable, the hens and the asker differ between the two halves of one farm day, and the reveal as drawn can't be seen at 275 px. Against a map that says "small", it is three games, five backgrounds, two cameras per plant, a new scene, a new phase, a new speaking turn and a save feature that doesn't exist. Fix findings 1-7 with the farm-animals designer in one pass (one count rule, one cast, one openable, one art list), cut to visit 1 plus a one-scene orchard, and re-judge; then it is close.
