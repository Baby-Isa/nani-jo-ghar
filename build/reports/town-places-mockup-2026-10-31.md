## Prompt A: the map

```
I'm designing a children's language game (ages 3-8, played on phones and tablets). I need a MOCK-UP of one game screen so I can judge whether a child can recognise each place at a glance. Attached: three style references from the game. Match their look exactly: a stylised 3D animated-feature look, soft warm sunlight from the upper left, cream limewash walls, terracotta roofs, potted plants, chunky simplified shapes, clean surfaces, no outlines, no photorealism.

THE SCENE: a small, friendly town seen from above at a TILTED angle, like a board-game map or a toy town on a table, with the camera about 50 degrees down, NOT straight down. The front of every building must be visible, because a child recognises a place by its front, not its roof. Wide landscape, 16:9.

THE PLACES: 8 buildings, spread evenly over the map in two loose rows of four, with a simple looping road and pavements between them. Each must be recognisable with NO WRITING, by its shape, colour and one big prop:
1. Library: a big window full of colourful books, and a giant open book above the door.
2. School: a small bell tower, a little playground with a slide, and school bags by the gate.
3. Hospital: a clean white building with an ambulance outside and a big pink heart with a plaster on it above the door (no red cross).
4. Fire station: red, with big garage doors open and a fire engine inside.
5. Bakery: a window of bread loaves and round flatbreads, and a giant bread loaf above the door.
6. Fruit and vegetable shop: crates of oranges, bananas, tomatoes and green chillies out front under a striped awning.
7. Post office: a big red postbox outside and a giant envelope above the door.
8. Park: trees, a pond with ducks, swings, and a bench (no building).

THE PEOPLE ROW: along the bottom 18% of the image, a plain soft-green strip with 4 round portrait badges (head and shoulders, a white ring around each): a grandmother in a cream kameez and red dupatta, a grandfather in a navy blazer, a girl of about 6, and a young woman. Same render style as the people reference. They are placeholders, not real people.

SHOW ONE MOVE: a dotted path from the grandmother's badge to the library's door, and a small version of her walking along the road.

RULES: absolutely no text, letters, numbers, logos or signs with writing anywhere. Keep each place clearly separate, with space around it, and no clutter. Everything must still read when the whole image is shrunk to phone size (about 700 pixels wide).
```

**Second image, same chat:** "Now the same town with 12 places instead of 8: add a train station (platform and a small train), a toy shop (teddy bears and balloons in the window), a swimming pool, and a café (little tables outside). Keep everything else the same." This shows how many places fit before it gets too busy.

## Prompt B: the street

```
I'm designing a children's language game (ages 3-8, played on phones and tablets). I need a MOCK-UP of one game screen so I can judge whether a child can recognise each place at a glance. Attached: three style references from the game. Match their look exactly: a stylised 3D animated-feature look, soft warm sunlight from the upper left, cream limewash walls, terracotta roofs, potted plants, chunky simplified shapes, clean surfaces, no outlines, no photorealism.

THE SCENE: a sunny town street seen SIDE-ON at eye level, like the attached street reference, with the camera looking straight at the building fronts across a quiet road. Wide landscape, 2:1. Sky and a few soft hills at the top, the building fronts across the middle, the pavement in front of them.

THE PLACES: 5 buildings side by side, the same height band but clearly different shapes and colours, each with its door at pavement level and a little space between them. Each must be recognisable with NO WRITING, by its shape, colour and one big prop:
1. Library: a big window full of colourful books, and a giant open book above the door.
2. School: a small bell tower on the roof, a low fence with a slide just visible behind it, and school bags by the gate.
3. Hospital: a clean white building, an ambulance parked in front, and a big pink heart with a plaster on it above the door (no red cross).
4. Bakery: a window of bread loaves and round flatbreads, and a giant bread loaf above the door.
5. Fire station: red, with a big garage door open and a fire engine inside.

THE PEOPLE ROW: along the bottom 18% of the image, a plain soft-green strip with 4 round portrait badges (head and shoulders, a white ring around each): a grandmother in a cream kameez and red dupatta, a grandfather in a navy blazer, a girl of about 6, and a young woman. Same render style as the people reference. They are placeholders, not real people.

SHOW ONE MOVE: the grandfather, full body, small, walking along the pavement and just stepping through the bakery's open door, with a dotted line from his badge to him.

RULES: absolutely no text, letters, numbers, logos or signs with writing anywhere. Keep each building clearly separate and uncluttered. Everything must still read when the whole image is shrunk to phone size (about 700 pixels wide).
```

**Second image, same chat:** "Now a second street in the same style with five different places: a post office (big red postbox, giant envelope above the door), a fruit and vegetable shop (crates under a striped awning), a toy shop (teddy bears and balloons in the window), a café (little tables outside) and a train station entrance (an arch with a train just visible behind). Same people row."

## What to look for

- Shrink each image to about 700 px wide (a phone). Can you name every place within a second, without the prop above the door?
- Do any two places look alike?
- On the map: does 8 still read, and does 12 tip into busy?
- Are the people badges big enough to tap (about a thumb)?
