## Headline grammar
1. **Describing words agree:** *wadho* (he), *wadha* (he, more than one, even with *cup*), *wadhi* (she), and *wadhe* before "with / in / on" (*wadhe chokre sathe*, *wadhe ambe je mathe*). *lal* never changes.
2. **The *-e* form is a rule now**, not a tendency: *chokre, ambe, darwaje, bakre, rasore* before a postposition, and the describing word and "of" word follow (*Nani je ambe je mathe*).
3. **"of" agrees with the thing, never the owner:** *Nana jo cup, Nana ja cups, Nana ji maani*. Same for *munjo, tojo, anjo, injo, asanjo, panjo, iloka jo*.
4. **"be":** *aau … aiya, tu … aiye, aai … aayo, e … ai*, plural *ain*.
5. **Two "we"s:** *pa* (with you), *asa* (not you).
6. **"Good":** *dayo* (well-behaved), *saro/sari* (things), mostly English *fine*.
7. **Cooking verbs** (bare commands to a child): 17 of the 21, below.
