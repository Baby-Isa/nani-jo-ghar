# The three trips: farm, beach, shops (research, 19 Oct 2026)

**Conclusion first.** Build the **farm** as Day 2, the **shops** as the first rainy day (about Day 4), and the **beach** as the sunny day after Mum's next recording round. The farm wins because almost everything it needs exists: hen, goat and chick art (nine poses each); goats recorded with counts, owners and "with the goat"; eggs, corn, the vegetables, the car, "stop the car" and "don't run, walk"; and every farm game is a reskin of a built or designed engine. The shops run on Find it's built pipeline plus one new game (pay). The beach needs the most new words (sea, sand, shell, bucket, spade, kite, towel, sun hat are all to record), two new mechanics and a full art run, so it goes last with its own sprint. Three shared engines serve all three trips and come first: **put it in its place** (Tidy up's place engine on the core), **count to a heard number** (Cook's count generalised to eggs, coins, shells) and **the window belt** (the clinic's pharmacy belt as the view out of the car).

Legend: ✓ recorded by the family · (d) Kutchi known but unchecked · (tr) to record. Every Kutchi line below is English; nothing is invented.

**Count:** 18 games; 7 reused as is, 8 reskins, **3 new** (pay, sandcastle, kite). By verb the reskins collapse to three engines that serve all three trips and come first, on the core:

1. **Put it in its place** (Tidy up's place engine): feed the animals, animals to bed, the picnic cloth, the shopping away, Nana's thing. Greyboxed already, and it is the Birthday's set-the-table too.
2. **Count to a heard number** (Cook `count` for any item into any container): eggs, cobs, coins, cones, shells. Pay is this with a price line.
3. **The window belt** (the clinic belt over a scrolling background): one file, three views.

Then pay, the chicks (designed), the sandcastle; the kite later.

**Leak checks.** Pay: coins unnumbered, the price never on screen, two prices in order from L2. Sandcastle: three bucket sizes, the order heard decides; a wrong stack still stands (no verdict) and the review shows it. Window: look-alikes pass together, so waiting or tapping everything scores nothing. Packing: the weather never picks the item alone.

## 4. Order, and decisions for Zafar

**Order:** Day 1 kitchen → **Day 2 farm** (sun) → Day 3 kitchen with the farm's eggs → **Day 4 shops** (rain; buys the kite and Big Ma's cloth) → Day 5 clinic (cloud) → **Day 6 beach** (sun, Masi, the kite) → Day 7 sewing with the cloth. The souvenirs make each day set up the next without a plot.

1. **Farm first, then shops, then beach.** Recommend yes (the art and words exist; the beach needs a recording round and an art run).
2. **The farmer is Kaka and Kaki** (a family farm), not a generic farmer. Recommend yes: kinship words and the respect register for free; the cast already describes them.
3. **The beach day is a visit to Masi**, and ask whether Masi will record her own lines. Recommend yes.
4. **The door's weather is a picture (the sky) plus Nani's line;** spoken English only on Days 1-2. Recommend yes.
5. **The packing leak rule:** weather sets the category, Kutchi picks the item and count. Recommend yes, as a check in `checks-to-build.md`.
6. **Transport:** car to the farm (*car* ✓ and *stop the car* ✓ exist), bus to the beach, on foot to the shops. Recommend yes.
7. **Money:** picture coins with no digits; prices are the numbers 1-4 plus whatever word Mum uses for money (shilling? paisa?). Recommend: ask Mum, never guess; the game ships with numbers only until then.
8. **The child's one free buy** (*take one yourself* ✓) at the shops, and the kite is what sets up the beach. Recommend yes.
9. **Beach food:** corn, cassava chips and coconut water instead of fried doughnuts. Recommend yes (savoury, all three words recorded).
10. **The farm's food is a picnic under the tree**, not a farm kitchen. Recommend yes: it gives Tidy up's place engine a second home and the cloth is the Birthday's table again.
11. **Build the three shared engines first** (place, count-to-a-number, the window belt), then pay, the chicks, the sandcastle; the kite later; milking and paddling cut. Recommend yes.
12. **Nana stays home every trip and gets the day's thing** (*This is for Nana* ✓ is recorded). Recommend yes.
13. **A Round 6 recording sheet, "Trips":** the (tr) words above (about 40: weather, farm, beach, shop and bag nouns, the money word, sandals and shoes) plus the three door lines and "we've come to see the animals, can we help?". Recommend: send it with the farm sprint so the beach is unblocked when the farm ships.

**Cost.** Farm: wiring, three reskins, one small art run (the farm background, the picnic tree). Shops: Find it plus pay on the bazaar background that exists. Beach: its own art run (background, stall, Masi's sheet, bus, kite).
