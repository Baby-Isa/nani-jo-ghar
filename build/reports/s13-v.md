## What changed

- **Step 0, the probes.** The sandbox's browser has no voice, so device-voice lines always looked heard. New `build/sandbox/lib/device-voice.mjs` models a phone's voice on pages with an `utter` capture: a queue, voices that load lazily, and a cancel that drops what waits. The voice timeline marks a line `[silent]` or `[cut]`; new expect `heardWhole:life:request`. CK-48 films 24 frames at 200 ms from the tap through the card, on Lighthouse's mobile run (1.6 Mbit/s, 150 ms, 4x CPU); each frame's edge is marked drawn or not. Hooks: `lib/sound.mjs`, `lib/evidence-probe.mjs`.
- **LNG-16.** `js/core/voice.js` asks for the voices on load and waits for them, so the opening word is no longer spoken before they are in.
- **Task 1.** `window.NjgVoice` (in voice.js) now plays every clip and device word for the clinic kit, conversations, story, results, the help kit and Cook's fallback TTS. On the store path only OK family clips play, never the device voice. The copies are gone. New static check `voice`.
- **Task 3.** `family-voice.js` and `day.js` share the core's one family-audio fetch.
- **Task 4.** Cook's effects and the boing now live in `js/shared/sfx.js`, by name.
- **Task 5.** index, first and labs now load `js/core/save.js` (classic scripts deferred after it).
