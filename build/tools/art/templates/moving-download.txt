MOVING IMAGES (references in, kept images stay put until the end)
Page JavaScript fetch() and the clipboard are blocked on chatgpt.com and raw.githubusercontent.com, so don't use them. ChatGPT shows its generated images as in-page blob: addresses that nothing outside the page can fetch, so never try to copy one out with page JavaScript or to upload it anywhere; last time that was refused and the run lost its pictures.

1. INTO CHATGPT (references). Fetch each reference once into your cloud workspace (with its file name) and keep it there for the whole run. In a fresh ChatGPT chat use your file-upload tool to attach exactly the files a prompt's line names, and check the composer shows exactly that many thumbnails before you paste.

2. INTO CHATGPT (a kept image, "kept:X"). It is still in the chat where it was made. In the new prompt's fresh chat press the "+" button in the composer, choose "Add from library", pick that image (the newest in that chat), and check the thumbnail is the kept one, not an attachment of its chat. Never download it and re-upload it.

3. OUT OF CHATGPT: only at the very end, with ChatGPT's own download button, in the run order (see AT THE END). Until then every kept image stays in its chat, and its chat link and save-as name are in THE LOG.
