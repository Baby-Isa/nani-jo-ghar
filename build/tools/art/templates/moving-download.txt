MOVING IMAGES (references in, kept images stay put until the end)
Page JavaScript fetch() and the clipboard are blocked on chatgpt.com and raw.githubusercontent.com, so don't use them. ChatGPT shows its generated images as in-page blob: addresses that nothing outside the page can fetch, so never try to copy one out with page JavaScript or to upload it anywhere; last time that was refused and the run lost its pictures.

1. INTO CHATGPT (references): the screenshot route (Zafar, 5 Nov: "it should get images it needs from github"). Your upload tool refuses files you fetched yourself ("Files you created cannot be uploaded"), but it does take your own screenshots, so a reference goes in as a screenshot of itself. Never ask Zafar to attach, upload or drag anything, and never stop the run for a reference: this route always works.
   a. Open the reference's RAW address (WHERE FILES LIVE) in its own tab and wait until the picture shows whole. Chrome shows a picture on its own on a dark background: a small one at its own size in the middle, a large one shrunk to fit the tab. Leave the zoom at 100%.
   b. Take a screenshot of that tab and note its screenshot id against the reference's name (e.g. "option1.png = screenshot 12"). Keep that tab open for the whole run and reuse the same screenshot for every prompt that names the reference; take it again only if the id stops working.
   c. In the prompt's fresh ChatGPT chat, upload the screenshots into the composer with your image-upload tool (onto the message box as a drop target, or the composer's own file input), exactly the references the line names, in the order it names them. Check the composer shows exactly that many thumbnails before you paste.
   d. Paste the prompt, then add this one sentence at its end: "Each attached picture is a browser screenshot of the reference: use only the picture itself and ignore the dark border around it."

2. INTO CHATGPT (a kept image, "kept:X"). It is still in the chat where it was made. In the new prompt's fresh chat press the "+" button in the composer, choose "Add from library", pick that image (the newest kept try of that chat, by its title and thumbnail), and check the thumbnail is the kept one, not an attachment of its chat. Never download it and re-upload it.
