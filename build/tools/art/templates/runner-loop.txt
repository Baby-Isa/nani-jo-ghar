THE RUNNER LOOP (decision 43). This is why the run is fast: read it twice.
A window is one ChatGPT chat in its own browser tab. Keep {{WINDOWS}} windows generating at all times. A window must never sit with a finished picture and no next prompt. Anything that is not scanning, judging or sending ({{CHORE_NAMES}}) happens only while every window is generating.

YOUR NOTES (update after every action). {{NOTES}}

THE TICK. Repeat until every line is {{DONE}}:
1. SCAN. Look at every generating tab (a screenshot or its page text, never the chat history). Note which are finished, still generating, stalled or errored.
2. HARVEST each finished tab, first finished first. Judge it now, in one look at the full image against its check and the PASS/FAIL LIST (no waiting, no second opinions):
   PASS: mark it KEPT; every WAITING prompt that needed it becomes READY now. {{PASS_SAVE}}
   FAIL with redos left: {{LOOP_REDO}}
   FAIL with no redos left: keep the best of the tries, note what is wrong, and treat it as a pass for the queue.
   A REVIEW is two messages, never one (Zafar, 10 Nov): first ask ChatGPT only for its opinion of the image against the house brief, no image wanted; then paste its analysis back, attach the image and its references, and ask explicitly: "Make a new image that applies this feedback." Asked for both in one message it often answers with words only; never wait for an image you did not ask for in that message.
   STUBBORN (a redo comes back nearly unchanged, as with sauces and dough): do not attach the image it is stuck on again. Open a brand new chat and ask, in plain words that never mention the game, for a realistic photo of just the thing (e.g. "a glass bowl of flour and water, half mixed into a shaggy dough, realistic photo"). Then, in a game chat with the house brief, attach that photo plus an empty container (or the bare item) and a different kept image to show the house style, and ask for the game version. Log both images; the photo is a reference, not a pick.
3. FILL. While fewer than {{WINDOWS}} windows are generating and a prompt is READY, START the first READY prompt in the free tab: new chat, attach the references its line names as MOVING IMAGES says (check the composer: exactly that many thumbnails), paste the prompt with its slots filled, send. Leave {{GAP}} seconds between sends, and spend that gap on one chore from step 4, not on waiting. Order of READY: redos first; then the prompt that unblocks the most WAITING ones (a sheet, a W1, a K1, a Y1, a C1, a D1); then the next in the run order. The parts are priorities, not walls: a free window takes any READY prompt, even from a later part.
4. HOUSEKEEP, only when every window is generating, one chore at a time, then straight back to step 1: {{CHORES}} No chore may start while a tab is finished and unharvested, and none may take more than about two minutes.

STALLS. A tab is stalled when its screen shows no change (no progress text, no image) for {{STALL}} minutes, or it shows an error ("Something went wrong", a network error, an empty reply).
- First, reload that chat once: the picture is often finished and the page is stuck.
- Still nothing {{HARD}} minutes after the send: abandon that chat and run the same prompt (same attachments) in a fresh chat, once. This retry does not use up a redo.
- That retry stalls too: SKIP the prompt. Log "STALLED: <id> | <time>", log every prompt that waited for it as "SKIPPED: waits for <id>", free the slot and carry on.
- Never leave a stalled tab holding a slot while others are idle.
