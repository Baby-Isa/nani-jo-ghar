build/tools/art/data/wada-colors.json: Sanzo Wada, A Dictionary of Color Combinations (159 colours, 348 combinations),
vendored verbatim on 6 Nov 2026 from the open transcription the palette review used (build/reports/s17-palette-review.md):
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mattdesl/dictionary-of-colour-combinations/master/colors.json
  repository https://github.com/mattdesl/dictionary-of-colour-combinations at commit c142bd0bc8049ea48db4da5eb397981f047e8ef4
  (a fork of Dain M. Blodorn Kim's dblodorn/sanzo-wada data; CMYK converted to sRGB with U.S. Web Coated SWOP v2 to sRGB).
artcolour.py reads only name, combinations and hex; it computes its own Lab from hex (D65) so it matches the pixels it measures.
