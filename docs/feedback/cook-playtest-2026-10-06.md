# Cook play-test, 6 Oct 2026: every point, the causes in code, and the decisions

**Source:** Zafar's four voice notes after the Sprint 1 publish (live build `20261006T012012Z`): story days on Cook's home first, then single stations in `labs.html`. Transcripts (Whisper drafts; Kutchi spellings are guesses): `docs/archive/feedback-transcripts/cook-playtest-2026-10-06-transcript-part1.md` … `-part4.md` (26 + 25 + 26 + 15 min).

Times are **part:m:ss**. Causes were checked by reading the code (file:line); nothing was run in a browser. **[Z]** = needs Zafar's yes (§4); **[Art]** = the art run; **[Mum]** = Mum's next round. Nothing here is built until Zafar answers §4 (rule A1).

**One thing to know first (it explains several "I don't know what game this is" moments, 2:24:19, 3:11:15, 4:0:27):** the numbers 1–6 on Cook's home are the six **story days**, not levels (`js/cook/flow.js:1003-1010`). Levels 1–4 live only in the Station lab ("?" → Station lab) and `labs.html`. A story day chains the pantry, an order, the "pass me" interruptions and the next customer, so it felt long and repetitive.
