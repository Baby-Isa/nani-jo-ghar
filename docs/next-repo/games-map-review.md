## 3. The rebalance

**The lopsidedness is not quite where it looks.** Food decides 20 games, but food is 90 words: fair. The real imbalance is per word to learn:

- **Over-served:** sizes (12 games for 16 words), numbers (19 for 40), positions (11 for 28).
- **Starved:** doing words (5 games for 100 words), describing words (9 for 60), time (2 for 35), school (1 for 30), toys (1 idea for 22), sports and jobs (none).

The rule I used: **where a game has a second thing that could decide, let it decide at a later level, and let number and size step back there.** Level 1 never changes, so the fun comes first and no word is a toll. No mechanic is removed.

**Against the targets**

- **Every group that is in now decides somewhere and is heard in three or more.** Toys, sports and jobs are no longer gaps.
- **Still thin:** wild animals, school and routines sit exactly on the floor. Per word, doing words are still furthest behind (10 games for 100 words), then describing, home, health and time: add levels there next, not games.
- **Toys** lean on Ali's play room (already suggested) for half their cover. Build it.
- **Out groups** stay out. The birthday's own words (cake, candle, present) are already heard at the party.
- **Load.** 66 of the 75 are inside the band. Outside: Find it and Pack the bag (nine groups, but each trip brings only its share), the first heal row (five mini-games in one row), and six small scenes and engines, thin by nature.
- **None of it plays until Mum records it:** the jobs, sports, tools and amounts are new asks for the recording sheet.

## 5. Top five recommendations

1. **Say "24 games in 75 skins" and plan by the mode.** Build the six big engines to chai's standard, one at a time; each then yields six to eight games for the price of settings and art.
2. **Make the eight merges.** Four are parked greyboxes with successors already.
3. **Move the weight from counting to doing.** Let doing words decide in eggs, chop, the heal games and the farm photos: the biggest gain for the curriculum, and funnier.
4. **Home jobs, sports and toys inside games that exist** (the town map, the waiting room, the pet hotel, the bags, the presents). Add one game only, the park.
5. **Keep level 1 untouched everywhere.** Each change is a later level, and each needs the leak test before it is built: two choices alike but for the new word.

**Yours to decide (yes to all except...):** 1. the eight merges; 2. the rebalance table as the plan for later levels; 3. the park as the one new game; 4. Ali's play room confirmed; 5. the out groups stay out.
