# Cook with Nani: can you win without the Kutchi?

**Date:** 24 Sept 2026 · **Feeds:** Waves 2 and 3 in `docs/cook-with-nani-todo.md`

## What this is

The owner's wife asked one question: can someone who knows **no Kutchi** earn the ear star ("understood") or play well just by reading, spotting patterns, remembering fixed recipes, or ruling out the wrong answers? This audit goes through every current station and every planned redesign with that question in mind.

The design doc (section 13) says most of play now depends on the Kutchi. The code agrees about the *words* that get said. It does not agree about **what the game shows, skips or finishes for you**. The four biggest holes:

1. **Help that shows the answer doesn't cost the ear star.** Wait about 5–12 s and Nani says the word, then 4 s later the right item glows. You tap it and keep the ear star. Translate (English) on any order line or on "pass me" also keeps it. In Busy mode none of this costs anything, because the lightning star is only about patience.
2. **The game makes some of the decisions for you.** If the order says "no milk" or "no sugar", that stove step is simply skipped. The number of cups is poured for you. Chips are fried only when they were ordered. For chopping, the round ends by itself when you reach the number. So the only place a "no X" or a count is tested is somewhere else, or nowhere.
3. **The screen gives the answer away.** The mission card's step chips say "Milk", "Extra", "Sugar" and "Chips" only when those were ordered. The fry tray always holds exactly one more than you need. The stir speed dial only appears when a speed was asked for, and its green zone sits where that speed is. The samosa screen says "Samosa 2 of 2".
4. **Some slots never change.** Daal always chops onions and chaat always chops potatoes. Chaat always starts with chickpeas. Mishkaki always starts with meat and always has 4 pieces. The chai tea step is always tea leaves. The samosa decoy is always "the other main filling". Each customer's tastes are fixed.

Not covered: English placeholder words (the family's words are coming). Already fixed: count badges show a running tally, not the target. English placeholders are never dotted out. At stage 4, item labels keep a speaker button.

**How the ear star works now (code):** `ctx.listen(false)` is called only when you tap a wrong item, cut a wrong vegetable, get the wrong count at countIn, roll, stir or fry, get the stir speed wrong, or miss a "pass me". Nothing else takes it away. Hints and translations call `Cook.onHelp`, which costs only the "no help" tick in Relaxed mode and costs **nothing** in Busy mode (`flow.js` lines 83–86 and 248).

Severity: **High** means a non-speaker keeps the ear star or plays well almost every time. **Medium** means it works often or in some orders. **Low** means an edge case or a missed opportunity. Wave: **2** is shared systems, **3** is stations.

## Top 8 fixes (ranked by learning impact per effort)

1. **Help that shows the answer costs the ear star.** Right now, waiting for the glow, the glow after 2 misses, and translate all keep it. Hearing a word again costs only the no-help star. In Busy mode help must cost something too. *(Wave 2, a few lines in `stations.js step`, `flow.js onHelp`, `UI.passMe`.)* This one fix closes the biggest route to a free ear star.
2. **Step chips the same for every order of a dish.** No "Milk", "Sugar", "Extra" or "Chips" chip only when ordered. *(Wave 2, tiny.)*
3. **Stop hiding the decisions: every optional step is always offered.** The milk jug and sugar bowl at the chai stove every time; the chips basket after every grill; spare cups; the "no X" item always on the pantry shelf. The player chooses to add or skip, and the ear star grades it. Also say "with milk" out loud. *(Wave 3, mostly in the Chai tray build.)* This is what makes "no" and "none" real Kutchi decisions.
4. **Stop showing the count.** Fry tray count + 1–3 at random; drop "Samosa 2 of 2"; chop doesn't end by itself at the number, and extra slices count against you; no one-slot-per-unit layouts in the ladder, Chai tray, Maani line or grill. *(Wave 3, plus the ladder rule in Wave 2.)*
5. **Stir dial with fixed bands and no target zone.** Always shown, so only Nani's *slowly/quickly* decides. The same rule applies to the half/full fill lines in the Chai tray. *(Wave 3, small.)*
6. **Unfreeze the fixed slots and add decoys.** Vary the chop target, the first chaat layer, the first mishkaki piece and its length; 2–3 random decoys in assemble, fill and thread; shuffle the samosa decoy; salt sometimes in the tadka. *(Wave 3, mostly data.)*
7. **Order ladder that gives nothing away by its shape.** No pictures or colours; one dot per item, not per unit; "no" rows placed at random and styled like the others; random order for any-order rows; at higher levels "ne poi" alone marks the sequence. Put numbers on other items too, so a row with a number isn't always sugar. *(Wave 2.)*
8. **Counting aloud and label speakers fade by word stage.** At number stage 3+ count silently; track number words in every counted station. At stage 3+, tapping the target's label speaker counts as help. *(Wave 2, small.)* The ear star then needs the meaning, not just matching sounds.

Next after these: symmetric look-alike groups for "pass me" (Wave 2, data only); tastes as a bias, with "not today" changes; the ear star checking who each dish is for (Chai tray first).
