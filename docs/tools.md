# Tools

Every tool in one table. Run one when the job needs it; each prints its own `--help`. The rules that used to say "run X before Y" now live in the tools themselves where they can (decision 99).

## Art and audio

`python3 build/tools/art/artcolour.py <files or folders> [--sheet S.png]` measures any picture's colours against Sanzo Wada's palette (one combination per item; a colour the book lacks is Zafar's exception) and the house family, its light, edge halo, baked shadow (soft or opaque), size class, camera height and its batch's odd ones out, and says whether a page or data file uses it (art-pipeline.md section 15; the art judge and every paste block use it; `--changed` runs in `checks.mjs` as `art`). `build/tools/art/`, `build/tools/audio/` and the `build/*.py` scripts cut, fit and check art and audio (most were one-off cuts for a past art run; `/art-run` and `/mum-round` name the ones they use). Skills live in `.claude/skills/`; the `judge` agent in `.claude/agents/`. `python3 build/tools/art/artdespill.py <cut-outs>` takes the magenta ground's pink off a cut-out's rim (only within 3 px of its edge), the pass after `artcut.py` on a magenta sheet (stove landing, 6 Nov). Since Fable's re-tune (6 Nov) it merges a material's tones, treats warm steel and stone as neutral, allows one small accent, judges natural things by natural bands and skies by the sky rule, and skips people. `python3 build/tools/art/artredo.py SCAN.json --rescore --yaml Y --csv C` re-cuts the redo list from a scan; `python3 build/tools/art/recolour.py [--find] [--sheet S]` pulls the generator's sky blue to Olympic Blue on its listed toothbrush and plaster pictures (`-v2` beside the old).
