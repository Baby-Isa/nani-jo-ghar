## The rules (decide these first)

| # | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **R1** | **The card is what's wanted.** Its headline is the goal in the asker's words (*Muke chai khape.*; clinic: [Let's clean it and heal it]). Its rows are the items or steps, ticking as they're done. The card never changes its words mid-round; it only ticks, lights and folds. | One place to look for "what did they ask for". |
| **R2** | **The guide box is the next step, one at a time, in step with the highlight.** A short line ("Chulo bar!") changes the moment the step changes. It may name the next item, but it **never reads the whole card**. At L1 it speaks as each step opens. From L2 it stays quiet and speaks only after a pause (help), with the glow. When there's nothing to do, it's empty. | It's the recipe voice. No more two faces saying the same thing. |
| **R3** | **Bubbles belong to faces.** Someone on screen speaks from a bubble beside their face (above or below, where there's room). A guide who is off screen (Nani at a station, the doctor in the heal games) speaks in the guide box at the top left. Dialogue between characters is always in bubbles, never in the box. | Zafar, 6 Oct. |
| **R4** | **One face per person.** When the asker is also the guide (Nani in the pantry, Nani's chop list), there is **one card with her face**. Her next-step line shows as the card's top strip, not in a second box with her face again. | "You see the same face twice… it feels wrong." |
| **R5** | **No written English for the child anywhere.** The floating verdict words ("Perfect!", "Burnt!", "Golden!", "Too early", "Boiled over!"), the "Served!" stamp, and buttons like "Fry them." and "To the grill." become **pictures or a recorded Kutchi word**. English stays only behind "?". | Non-negotiable 5. Found by this inventory: they are live now. |
| **R6** | **Wrong moments use the family's sounds.** *arre re* goes; Mum's "oh oh oh" is used once at L1 on a real mistake, never on a right move. | Decision 61; the bug Zafar heard. |

**R4, the alternative:** keep two boxes, with the guide box showing no face (a small bulb or pot icon) when Nani is also the asker. *Recommend R4 as written:* one card is calmer.
