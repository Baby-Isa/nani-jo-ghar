These are pieces for a premium children's cooking game (ages 3-6) in a grandmother's kitchen. The 00-HOUSE pictures are the style:
Pixar-inspired, semi-photorealistic 3D, "stylised shapes with realistic materials", premium and polished, warm and calm. Copy their
style, not their contents: ignore the faces, labels, pills and the gear icon in them, and ignore the thin coloured guide lines and
small face stickers in any reference picture. Rules for every image:
- One large soft light from the upper left; nothing floats. The 00-HOUSE pictures have faint window-light streaks across the
  worktop; do NOT copy those: everything is evenly lit, no window streak.
- Materials: brushed stainless steel reflecting a neutral pale-grey room; cream ceramic (the bowls in F1 are THE bowl: the same
  shape, cream and rim in every bowl you draw); dark walnut only where a line asks; white marble worktop with soft cool grey veins
  only where a line asks for marble (never golden or cream marble).
- Camera: "straight down" means a flat plan view with no perspective at all: a bowl, a plate or a cob's end is a perfect circle, a
  tray a perfect rectangle, and no inner or outer side wall of any container shows, only its rim and its floor. Depth shows only
  as a soft shadow inside the rim along its upper-left inner edge. Any steel rim is ONE flat band of even width, no inner line,
  ledge or bevel. The reference pictures are sometimes tilted; yours must not be. "Side-on" lines are the exception.
- These are separate PIECES that we cut out and place in our own layout; we place the stove, the trays, the plates, the shadows and
  the word labels ourselves. Draw only what the line asks, nothing else: no stove, hob, knobs or flames, no hands, no text, letters,
  numbers or labels, no tools that do nothing, no napkins or plants unless asked, no steam, no glow, nothing in the air.
- Counts are exact: if a line says six, draw six, and count them in your check.
- Fill the frame: the objects asked for take up most of the picture, with a margin of about one tenth of the picture all round.
  Never small objects in a large empty field. Sizes are given as shares of the picture's width: keep to them.
- "On magenta" means one flat solid #FF00FF background edge to edge, no gradient, no texture, and NO shadow at all: no contact shadow,
  no soft shadow, nothing darker than the flat magenta anywhere (we add every shadow in code). The object must not touch the edges.
  We cut along the magenta, so it must not tint, reflect in or bleed onto the object: steel reflects a neutral pale-grey room, never
  pink; between thin pieces (strands, rings) the magenta stays pure.
- Where a line asks for several panels or rows: equal panels, the same camera, scale and light in each, separated only by a strip of
  the same background; no frames, borders, dividing lines or numbers.
- No oval plates or trays: round, square or rectangular only. No sweets or biscuits anywhere.
