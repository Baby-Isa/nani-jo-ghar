YOUR NOTES (update after every action). One line per prompt with its state: WAITING (a dependency isn't kept yet) · READY · GENERATING (tab, time sent) · KEPT (judged, logged with its chat link) · SKIPPED or STALLED (why). One queue: READY (redos at the front, then run order).

PASS/FAIL LIST (every image; any one of these is a fail)
- Any text, letters, numbers, labels, signs or logos, even fake or blurry ones.
- Outlines, cel shading, a flat vector look or painterly brushwork.
- The wrong number of things or the wrong view; a pan or glass seen even a little from the side (the inside wall wider on one side, the floor off centre); a hand, a sink or a side view copied from the photos.
- A key background that isn't one flat colour (a gradient, a floor, shadows or haze on it).
- In the kitchen, the island, window or door moved; anything new added; a person.
- Anything scary; sweets, lollies or biscuits; any religious marker.
