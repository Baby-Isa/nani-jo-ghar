CHECKS (by check ID)

PASS/FAIL LIST (every image; any one of these is a fail)
- Any text, letters, numbers, labels, logos or price stickers, even fake ones, on any box, bottle, tube, packet, dial or screen.
- Outlines, cel shading, a flat vector look or painterly brushwork.
- A needle, a blade or anything sharp pointed at the viewer; blood.
- Sweets, lollies, lollipops, biscuits or chocolates as anything (a sticker sheet is stars only).
- The generator's bright sky blue (a light electric cyan-blue): a blue is a calm mid Olympic Blue, and only where the line's prompt names one.
- A shadow, a reflection, a floor or a table under any item; anything crossing into the next cell; two items merged.
- A cell holding the wrong item, an item missing or drawn twice, or the order changed.
- Light oak or pine: any wood is walnut, not too dark on a lid.
- Any symbol or emblem on any item (a plain cross, a crescent or any sign): the items are plain.

HOUSE LOOK (every image; judge it with the checks above)
- House colours, from Sanzo Wada's A Dictionary of Color Combinations (combinations 283, 249, 311 and 200): Ochre Red (brick red), Carmine Red (dark red), Pompeian Red (dark red), Hay's Russet (maroon), Pale Burnt Lake (maroon), Sulpher Yellow (pale soft yellow), Ivory Buff (cream), Cream Yellow (light ochre), Olive Buff (light soft yellow), Ecru (stone), Olive Ocher (ochre), Yellow Ocher (light ochre), Cinnamon Rufous (cinnamon), Khaki (khaki), Dark Greenish Glaucous (light greyish green), Light Green Yellow (light yellow), Chromium Green (soft green), Venice Green (teal), Dark Medici Blue (dark greyish teal). Each picture's main colours come from ONE Wada combination, in lighter or darker tones (white, greys and near-black are free); a picture that mixes colours from different combinations, or has a colour the book lacks, is redone (Zafar, 6 Nov). Wood (lids, boards, handles, trays) is walnut, never light oak or pine.
- Edge finish: clean edges, no white halo, no rim light, no baked or drop shadow; light from the upper left.
