The block: `s19-tray-pot-chrome-block.txt`. References: `sources/art/s19-tray-pot/refs.zip`.

1. **T1, the empty stone tray.** It is his flat chaat-mock slab (ART-72), and it becomes the one shared tray.
   - It is seen from above, under the bowls and pills of corn, chop, daar, maani, sekelo and samosa.
   - It is cut into three slices: the two ends keep their corners and are never scaled; the middle is tiled from T2.
   - Code adds a faint, tight shadow, heavier on the lower right (Fable).
2. **T2, the same stone as a seamless tile.** It fills the middle at any width, so the grain never smears.
3. **P1 and P2, the top-down pot.** It is chai's saucepan, drawn exactly from above, with water and empty.
   - It is tried first in eggs (CK-74).
   - If it looks better, chai gets it as well, and chai's other pan states (milk, tea, leaves, the drain frames) need their own run or code-drawn contents. If not, neither game changes.
4. **E1 to E3, the eggs at the table.** These replace the old proto egg cup and fried egg on the served plates (Zafar, 10 Nov: "put it in the number 3 art one").
   - E1 is the cream plate seen at the table's angle. E2 is a fried egg and E3 a boiled egg's two halves, at the same angle and scale, so code lays one, two or more on a plate.
   - They match the cream plates and halved eggs on the hob screen.
