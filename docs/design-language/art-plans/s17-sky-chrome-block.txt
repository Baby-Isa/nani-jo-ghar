PASS/FAIL LIST (every image; any one of these is a fail)
- Any text, letters, numbers, labels, logos or watermarks, even fake ones; any drawing of the colour strip or swatches.
- Ground, hills, trees, birds, buildings or people in an only-sky picture.
- A flat cartoon blue, a loud saturated gradient, a neon or electric colour, or a dull lifeless grey where the line asks for colour.
- Colours from outside the attached strip (other than whites and soft greys): a green sky, a pink-purple night, a lemon sun.
- Drawn rain, lightning, rays as lines or spikes, lens flare, a face on the sun or moon.
- Anything busy in the upper LEFT quarter (the game's speech bubble sits there).
- In an edit: anything other than the sky moved, redrawn or recoloured.
- Any symbol or emblem in the sky: the moon is only the moon, with nothing beside it.
