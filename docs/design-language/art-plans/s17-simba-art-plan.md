Simba: Zafar's real cat, a Russian Blue with a dense, plush, short coat in a deep smoky grey with a soft warm taupe sheen, a solid round face, rounded cheeks, upright ears with pinkish-taupe insides, pale green eyes, a full fluffy tail. NO collar, no bell, no tag: his neck is plain fur.
The first attached picture is our approved sheet of Simba: copy his look, colours, coat, face and proportions EXACTLY, but draw NO collar.

Bottom row: (5) lying on his back, paws up, playful; (6) walking, side view facing left; (7) trotting, side view facing left, tail up; (8) sitting, licking one raised front paw.

Background: one perfectly flat, uniform light grey, hex #BDBDBD, filling the whole image edge to edge. No floor, no furniture except where a pose names one, no gradient, NO shadows on the grey, no colour swatches. Nothing touches an edge or another pose. Do not draw grid lines.

Style: the same stylised 3D animated-feature-film look as the attached sheet, soft fur detail, soft global illumination, warm light from the upper left, appealing and friendly, no outlines.
