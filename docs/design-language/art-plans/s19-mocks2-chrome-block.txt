=== HOUSE BRIEF (paste as the first message of each conversation) ===
These are screens from a premium children's cooking game (young children) in a grandmother's kitchen that teaches the family's language.
The 00-HOUSE pictures are the style we love: Pixar-inspired, semi-photorealistic 3D, "stylised shapes with realistic materials",
premium and polished, warm and calm. In every image I ask for, keep to these house rules:
- One consistent large soft light from the upper left, a little behind the viewer's mid-ground; soft contact shadows under everything,
  falling to the lower right; nothing floats. No window-light streaks across the worktop.
- Materials: a white marble worktop with soft cool veins (never yellow or cream-tinted); mid-walnut wood with subtle grain, softly
  bevelled edges and a satin finish; brushed stainless steel; cream ceramic bowls (never walnut bowls, never glass bowls).
- Camera: things lying on the worktop are seen exactly from straight above. Things that stand up (bottles, jars, shakers, egg boxes)
  stand side-on on a pale stone shelf along the bottom, like the chai picture. Never mix the two on one surface.
- Things the child picks from lie along the bottom of the screen: side-on things on chai's pale stone shelf, top-down things in cream
  bowls on a long walnut tray (a thin raised rim like A2-corn-tray-06, with the quieter grain of A2-corn-tray-05). Under each item leave
  a small blank cream pill shape (we write the word in it later). Items in one even row, spaced for a child's finger.
- The main thing (the stove and its pan, the board, the grill) is as big and as tall as the screen allows; the screen is landscape 3:2
  and must also read on a wide, short phone, so keep everything important inside the middle 80% of the height.
- Serving trays and plates sit on the right. Where a person is being served, a small round face circle sits on the corner of their
  plate or tray, as in the chai picture.
- Leave out: any text or words (except digits where I ask for a counter), hands, tick or next buttons, back arrows, tutorial dimming or
  gold highlights, plants, napkins, spoons or pots that do nothing, and anything in the top-right corner (a settings button lives there).
