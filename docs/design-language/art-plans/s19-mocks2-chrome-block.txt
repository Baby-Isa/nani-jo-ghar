=== HOUSE BRIEF (paste as the first message of each conversation) ===
These are screens from a premium children's cooking game (ages 3-6) in a grandmother's kitchen that teaches the family's language.
The 00-HOUSE pictures are the style we love: Pixar-inspired, semi-photorealistic 3D, "stylised shapes with realistic materials",
premium and polished, warm and calm. In every image I ask for, keep to these house rules:
- One consistent large soft light from the upper left; soft contact shadows under everything, falling to the lower right; nothing
  floats. No window-light streaks across the worktop (ignore the streaks in the sekelo picture).
- Materials: a white marble worktop with soft cool veins (never yellow or cream-tinted); mid-walnut wood with subtle grain, softly
  bevelled edges and a satin finish; brushed stainless steel; cream ceramic bowls (never walnut bowls, never glass bowls; the one
  glass thing allowed is a measuring jug where I ask for it).
- Camera: the worktop scene is seen exactly from straight above, in every game except baking (side-on throughout). Along the bottom
  of the screen runs ONE band, the bottom 30% of the height, of one of two kinds: chai's pale stone shelf, side-on, for things that
  stand up (bottles, cartons, jars, shakers, egg boxes); or a long walnut tray, straight-down, for things that lie or heap (cubes,
  cobs, dough, pastry, spices and sauces in cream bowls). Never both on one screen, never a tilted three-quarter view.
- The things the child picks from sit in that band: the tray has a thin raised rim like A2-corn-tray-06 with the quieter grain of
  A2-corn-tray-05; the sekelo picture's cream bowls are the right bowls (there they sit on a shelf; on ours they go on the tray).
  Under each item leave a small blank cream pill (we write the word in it later: the word pills in the 00-HOUSE pictures are ours,
  draw them blank). Items in one even row, spaced for a child's finger.
- The main thing (the stove and its pan, the board, the grill) is as big and as tall as the space above the band allows. Frame:
  wide 16:9 if you can, else 3:2. The sides get cropped on some screens, so keep everything important inside the middle 80% of the
  width; nothing important in the top-right corner (a settings button lives there). The picture is the play area only: a panel of
  ours sits to its left, outside the picture.
- Serving trays and plates sit on the right. Where a person is being served, a small round face circle sits on the corner of their
  plate or tray, as in the chai picture.
- Leave out: any text or words (except digits where I ask for a counter), hands, tick or next buttons, back arrows, tutorial dimming,
  gold highlights or glows, dotted guide circles, plants, napkins or cloths unless I ask, spoons or tools that do nothing, and anything
  falling through the air.
