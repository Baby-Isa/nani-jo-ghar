YOUR NOTES (update after every action). One line per prompt with its state: WAITING (a dependency isn't kept yet) · READY · GENERATING (tab, time sent) · KEPT (judged, logged with its chat link) · SKIPPED or STALLED (why). One queue: READY (redos at the front, then run order).

THE TICK. Repeat until every line is KEPT or SKIPPED:
