PASS/FAIL LIST (every image; any one of these is a fail)
- Any text, letters, numbers, labels or logos, even fake or blurry ones.
- Outlines, cel shading, a flat vector look, painterly brushwork or photorealism; blur, a vignette or lens flare.
- Pores, fine wrinkles, single hairs, fabric weave or knit, or refraction in glasses, on anyone (LA excepted on nobody: in LA the doctor must already be as smooth as Nani).
- The background isn't one flat mid-grey (sheets light blue-grey, the counter below Nani excepted); there's a floor, a surface or a shadow on the grey (a soft contact shadow under feet in a lineup is fine); grid lines, panel borders or labels.
- Anything touching an image edge, or crossing into a neighbour's cell on a sheet.
- The wrong number of people, people in the wrong order, or extra objects, people, hands or animals.
- Hands: not five fingers on each visible hand, fused or extra fingers.
- A lineup whose feet are not on one level, or whose heights are wrong: the doctor must be about 1.5 times the girl (her head top at his chest), Nani and Nana a little shorter than him.
- The girl not matching her sheet (face, plaits, ribbons, yellow dress, white leggings, white sandals) or looking older or younger (in LC and part C only the small rendering step asked).
- Skin that is orange, pink, grey or very pale instead of a warm light tan.
- Immodest or tight clothes on Nani, her dupatta off her head, or any religious marker (bindi, tilak, sindoor, deities, temple items).
- An edit that looks identical to the original, or where something other than what was asked moved, changed size or changed colour.
- Light from anywhere but the upper left, or a second light.
- A registered set (the doctor's poses on their s07 pictures, Nani's moods on N1B) whose figure moves or changes size between pictures.
- The doctor or Nani not clearly the same person as their attached picture (face shape, glasses, his beard and hairline, her dupatta's framing), or a likeness Zafar has not approved (real people: his yes is the pass).
- The doctor in a white coat, with a stethoscope or a bag, or without his glasses, beard, navy check blazer or silver watch; the watch on the wrong wrist.
