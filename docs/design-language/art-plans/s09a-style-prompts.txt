=== 1A. Nani's house: one wide arch ===
Make one landscape image, 3:2. Use the same style as the first attached picture: soft sunlit painted 3D look, warm light, gentle shadows, clean simple shapes, calm and uncluttered, made for young children. It should not look old-fashioned.
The scene is the front of Nani's house, seen from the path a few steps away, at eye level. The house is a warm family home with textured, rendered walls in cream and sand. Nani, the woman in the second attached picture, with the same face, clothes and proportions and fully covered modest clothing, stands in the porch, smiling and waving the viewer in.
