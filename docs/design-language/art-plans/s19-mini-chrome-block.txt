=== HOUSE BRIEF (paste as the first message of each conversation) ===
These are pieces for a premium children's cooking game (ages 3-6) in a grandmother's kitchen. The 00-HOUSE pictures are the style:
Pixar-inspired, semi-photorealistic 3D, "stylised shapes with realistic materials", premium and polished, warm and calm. Copy their
style, not their contents: ignore the faces, labels, pills and the gear icon in them. Rules for every image:
- One large soft light from the upper left; soft contact shadows falling to the lower right; nothing floats. The 00-HOUSE pictures
  have faint window-light streaks across the worktop; do NOT copy those: the worktop is evenly lit.
- Materials: white marble worktop with soft cool grey veins (the marble in the 00-HOUSE pictures, not a golden or cream marble);
  brushed stainless steel; cream ceramic bowls; wood only where a line asks.
- Camera: "straight down" means a flat plan view with no perspective at all: a bowl is a perfect circle, a tray a perfect
  rectangle, and no inner or outer side wall of any container shows, only its rim and its floor. Depth shows only as a soft shadow
  inside the rim. The 00-HOUSE pictures and G1 are tilted slightly; here they must not be. A line that says "side-on" is the
  exception.
- These are separate PIECES that we cut out and place in our own game layout; we place the stove, the plates and the word labels
  ourselves. So draw only what the line asks, nothing else: no stove, no hob, no knobs, no hands, no text, letters, numbers or
  labels, no tools that do nothing, no napkins, no plants, no steam, no glow or highlight effects, nothing falling through the air.
- Fill the frame: the objects asked for take up most of the picture, with a margin of about one tenth of the picture all round.
  Never small objects in a large empty field.
- "On a plain magenta background" means one flat solid #FF00FF colour edge to edge, no gradient, no texture, no shadow on it other
  than a very faint contact shadow directly under the object; the object must not touch the edges. We cut along the magenta, so the
  magenta must not tint, reflect in or bleed onto the object: steel reflects a neutral pale-grey room, never pink.
- Where a line asks for several panels: equal panels side by side, each the same camera, scale and light, separated only by a thin
  strip of the same background; no frames, borders, dividing lines or numbers.
- No oval plates or trays: round, square or rectangular only.
