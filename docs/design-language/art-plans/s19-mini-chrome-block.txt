=== HOUSE BRIEF (paste as the first message of each conversation) ===
These are pieces for a premium children's cooking game (ages 3-6) in a grandmother's kitchen. The 00-HOUSE pictures are the style:
Pixar-inspired, semi-photorealistic 3D, "stylised shapes with realistic materials", premium and polished, warm and calm. Rules for
every image:
- One large soft light from the upper left; soft contact shadows falling to the lower right; nothing floats. No window-light streaks.
- Materials: white marble worktop with soft cool veins; brushed stainless steel; cream ceramic bowls; wood only where a line asks.
- Camera: exactly straight down from above (a flat plan view, no perspective tilt, no side walls of containers visible beyond their
  rims) unless a line says side-on.
- These are separate PIECES that we cut out and place in our own game layout; we place the stove, the plates and the word labels
  ourselves. So draw only what the line asks, nothing else: no stove, no hob, no knobs, no hands, no text or letters, no tools that
  do nothing, no napkins, no plants, no glow or highlight effects, nothing falling through the air.
- "On a plain magenta background" means one flat solid #FF00FF colour edge to edge, no gradient, no texture, no shadow on it other
  than a very faint contact shadow directly under the object; the object must not touch the edges. We cut along the magenta.
- No oval plates or trays: round, square or rectangular only.
