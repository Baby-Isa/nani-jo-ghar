# Nani jo Ghar — Questions for Mum (Round 5: the sentences)

1 Oct 2026 · Thank you for Round 3! This round is built differently: instead of long word lists, it's mostly **short, everyday sentences**, the way you'd really say them in the kitchen. From these the game learns how Kutchi puts a sentence together, so that later it only needs to ask you for a new word, never for a new sentence. **The cooking game comes first** (it's the next thing we finish), then **the birthday party**, then the sentences that every later game is built on.

*Everything below is in English. You never need to explain any grammar: just say each line the way you'd say it to the person in brackets. The core is about 70 minutes, and there are "stop here" marks along the way, so it's fine to do it in two or three sittings.*

## How to answer

- **Record one long voice file** on the phone, in a quiet room. One sitting or several, it doesn't matter.
- **Before each part, say its name out loud** ("Part 2"), and before each numbered line say its number ("L12"). That's how the recording gets lined up with this sheet.
- **Word lists (Parts 1 and 3, and the party words in Part 5): say each line three times**, with a gap of about two seconds after each, the way you'd say it to a grandchild. The gaps let each one be cut out on its own.
- **Sentences (Parts 2, 4, 5 and 6): say each one once**, naturally, with a short gap after it. Where a line has several sentences separated by "·", say them one after the other, each once.
- **Say it the way you really would, not word for word.** If the English sounds odd in Kutchi, say what you'd actually say. If there are two ways, say both. **Not sure? Say "skip".** **If the family just uses the English word, say so:** that's a real answer.
- **When a set of sentences only changes one thing** (a boy, then a girl; one cup, then two), keep the rest of the sentence the same if that feels natural, so we can hear exactly what changes.
- The right-hand columns are for Zafar to fill in afterwards.

**Rough times:** Part 1 about 6 minutes · Part 2 about 10 · Part 3 about 12 · Part 4 about 10 · Part 5 about 10 · Part 6 about 25 (with stops inside) · then "if there's time".
