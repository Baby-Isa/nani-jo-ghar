## SECTION 1: THE CORE ARCHITECTURAL ENGINE MATRIX

The engine parses Kutchi sentences based on two distinct structural splits: An Aspect Split and a Person Split. The rule engine must evaluate structural paths using this hierarchy:

### Path A: Complete Agreement (Non-Perfective Aspect OR Intransitive Perfective)
*   **Trigger:** Clause aspect is progressive, future, habitual, OR perfective past but lacks a direct object (intransitive).
*   **Engine Instruction:** Resolve full phi-features against the Subject token.
*   **Target Features:** Person (π) + Number (#) + Gender (γ).

### Path B: Defective Agreement (Transitive Perfective + 2nd/3rd Person Subject)
*   **Trigger:** Clause aspect is perfective (completed action in the past), transitive (has a direct object), and the subject is 2nd or 3rd person.
*   **Engine Instruction:** Block the verb from checking Subject Gender (γ) and Singular Number.
*   **Target Features:** Verb falls back to track Object number and gender, or invokes the default Neuter ending if blocked.

### Path C: The 1st Person Split Exception (Transitive Perfective + 1st Person Subject)
*   **Trigger:** Clause aspect is perfective, transitive, and the subject is 1st person (`Aaũ` / `Asĩ`).
*   **Engine Instruction:** Invalidate subject tracking.
    *   *If 1st Person Singular ("I"):* Target Object Number and Gender strictly.
    *   *If 1st Person Plural ("We"):* Trigger a dialectal sub-routine (Forking behavior tracking either Subject number or Object features based on register constraint).

### 2.2 Suffix Paradigms for Regular Verbs (Root: *Khaa* [Eat])
*   **Present Progressive:** Suffix maps to Subject (M: `-nto`, F: `-nti`, Pl/Formal: `-nta-yo`).
*   **Future Simple:** Suffix maps to Subject (1Sg.M: `-ndos`, 1Sg.F: `-ndis`, 2Sg: `-ne`, 3Sg.M: `-ndo`).
*   **Transitive Past (Perfective):** Suffix bypasses Subject completely, matching the grammatical gender of the direct object (Masculine Object: `-do`, Feminine Object: `-di`, Neuter Object: `-dũ`).

### 2.3 Noun Pluralisation & Oblique Case Transformations
Before a postposition is applied, nouns undergo physical stem mutations (Oblique Case shifts):
*   **Masculine Nouns ending in *-o*:** Shift to *-aa* in plural, and mutate to *-e* in the singular oblique.
    *   *Direct Sg:* Chhokro (Boy) → *Oblique Sg:* Chhokre ke (To the boy).
*   **Feminine Nouns ending in *-i*:** Append *-yũ* in plural.
    *   *Direct Sg:* Chopdi (Book) → *Direct Pl:* Chopdiyũ (Books).
*   **Neuter Nouns ending in *-ũ*:** Shift to *-aa* in plural.
    *   *Direct Sg:* Marchũ (Chilli) → *Direct Pl:* Marcha (Chillies).

## SECTION 3: SYSTEMATIC NEGATION & POSTPOSITIONS

### 3.1 Binary Negation Routing
*   **Indicative Negation (Fact-Checking / Declaratives):** Appends to the end of descriptive phrases. Uses `Nathi` (નથી) or `Naay` (નાય).
*   **Imperative Negation (Prohibitions / Commands):** Prepended immediately before the verb root. Uses `Na` (ના) or `Ma` (મ).

### 3.2 Interrogatives & Relational Postpositions
*   **Interrogatives (Question Hooks):** `Kore` (What), `Kiyen` (How), `Kilaa` (Why), `Kere` (When), `Kerdhaan` (Where).
*   **Postpositions (Clitic Cases):** Always post-fixed to the oblique form of the noun token:
    *   Locative (In/On): `-mẽ` / `-te`
    *   Dative (To/For): `-ke` / `-laa`
    *   Ablative (From): `-vinũ` / `-maadhã`

## SECTION 4: THE FOUR ADVANCED SPEECH FLUIDITY ARCHITECTURES

### 4.1 Phonetic Implosive Mapping (For TTS Engine Interfacing)
Kutchi relies on four distinct **implosive consonants** where airflow is drawn inwards. The phonetic transcription module must convert native scripts using these mapping tags:
*   **ɓ** (બી - implosive b): Contrast *Bakar* (Goat) vs *ɓak-vuñ* (To babble).
*   **ɗ** (ડી - implosive d): Contrast *Daan* (Charity) vs *ɗeN-ũ* (To give).
*   **ʄ** (જી - implosive j): Contrast *Jaan* (Life) vs *ʄuñ-o* (Old).
*   **ɠ** (ગી - implosive g): Contrast *Gaal* (Cheek) vs *ɠaa-l* (Talk/Speech).

### 4.2 Differential Object Marking (DOM)
Animate objects trigger case-marking; inanimate objects suppress case-marking.
*   **Rule:** If `Object.animate == true`, append accusative enclitic `-ke`.
*   **Rule:** If `Object.animate == false`, assign null marker `Ø`.

### 4.3 Compound Verb Vectoring & Echo Reduplication
*   **Compound Verb Formula:** `[Primary Verb Root in Conjunctive Participle (-i)] + [Vector Root]` (*Khai Chhad* = Eat it all up).
*   **Echo-Word Reduplication Logic:** Extract word root. If word begins with consonant $C$, replace $C$ with $v$- or $m$- to form the secondary phrase string (*Chai-Vai* = Tea and snacks).
