# Zafar's thinking, 28 Sept 2026: story arcs, Nani's role, and focus

Status: **thinking, not decided.** Nothing here is built yet. Claude's view is in the chat of 28 Sept; the key points are summarised at the end.

## 1. Nani's role
Nani is less of a kitchen-bound character and more the **guide**: she helps the child learn, grow and explore.

## 2. Arcs 2 onwards: "a day out with Nani"
Each arc is a trip, built mostly from the existing modes, with new words each time.
- **The template:**
  1. Pack your bag (different items per trip).
  2. Cook your packed lunch.
  3. Travel: by bus, car or motorbike. The only art needed is the view out of the window. Nani says "spot the …", and you tap or photograph things as they pass (like the clinic's pharmacy conveyor).
  4. At the place: a food stall made of three mini-games (e.g. at the beach: corn on the cob, mishkaki, fried doughnuts).
  5. One or two games specific to the place: a sandcastle, a kite; a boat day could be its own trip.
  6. The recap by the fire (see 4).
- **First ideas for trips:** a beach day, a garden day, a safari day, a boat day.

## 3. Arc 1: a birthday party instead of Eid (Eid comes later: there's more to explain)
- The guests arrive, and each asks for their own food ("I'm cousin Ali, I'd like maani and saag"); you make it for them.
- Set the table.
- A sweets hide-and-seek, then pack the sweet box.
- Maybe Big Ma makes the outfit (instead of "something breaks and Big Ma fixes it").
- End by blowing out the candles.
- Dropped: greeting the guests at the door, the shoe mountain.

## 4. The end of each arc: Nani tells the day's story
- By the fire in the living room, Nani tells a short version of what happened that day: a picture storybook with her voice.
- It's built from what really happened that day (the things the child made and spotted).
- She pauses to ask questions ("Whose birthday was it?", "Which colour was it?"), and the child picks the answer.
- As the child progresses, they tell more of the story themselves.
- It reviews the day's words and builds the bond with Nani.
