# The sprint plan to the launch (Zafar, 2 Nov 2026: "we'll try stick to it this time")

**The launch:** every designed game, on the app stores (Arc 1, the Birthday, plus the day trips). Gujarati and his feedback rounds
come in the next big phase, after the launch. How a game goes from prototype to finished: `docs/process/prototype-to-game.md`.

## The rhythm: every sprint does two things

1. **Polish one reference game** with Zafar until he says "done": a nearly-done game where there is one, in a different family from
   the one being rolled out. While it is polished, its family's art block goes into the queue and its words onto Mum's sheet.
2. **Roll out last sprint's reference to its whole family:** every game in that family, prototype or nearly done, brought to the
   reference's standard on the shared kit, judged on film, then played by Zafar in one sitting.

Alongside, every sprint: the art queue keeps at least two days of paste blocks ready (ChatGPT's 120 images a day; Zafar runs them
when he can and judges while they run or after); Mum's recording sheet grows by family and goes to him when he sees her.

## Sticking to it

- New work goes into the sprint whose family it belongs to; anything else goes to `docs/ideas.md`. A sprint never takes a second goal
  (`/sprint`).
- This page changes only on Zafar's word, with the date in the row that moved.
- At each sprint's close: the chain row is marked done, with the commit on `main`, and the next sprint opens from this table.
