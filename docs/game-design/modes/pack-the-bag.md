# Pack the bag

**Designed; Sprint 10.** A reskin of Cook's pantry fetch (`cook.md`): a bag on the step, five to eight things on the floor, Nani names one at a time, tap it and it hops in. Used by every trip's pack stage (`docs/game-design/days/`).

## Rules

- **The leak rule:** the weather sets only the kind of thing (sun: a hat; rain: an umbrella); the Kutchi decides which one and how many (the big hat, the red one, two bottles), with decoys of the same kind, so the sky alone never picks the answer. To build as a check (`docs/process/checks-to-build.md`).
- The trip's things come from the trip file; what is packed reappears at the place (the hat on the child, the bucket holding the shells).
- Levels: L1 one thing at a time, named; L2 a count or a size; L3 two things in one line.
