# The shops

**The market with Nani, a rainy day (designed; after the farm).** Sources, newest wins: Zafar's lines of 19-21 Oct (`docs/archive/game-design/story-arcs-retired.md` "The 19 Oct paragraph", this file); the trip research (`build/reports/research-trips-2026-10-19.md` §1 "The shops") where Zafar hasn't changed it.

**Zafar's design (19 Oct):** a first-person maze of lanes, followed by spoken directions; each shop is its own game; paying in *paisa*; a goat steals something from the bag and people along the way say which way it went. Rain and umbrella beats are left out for now.
